# Playable canon — Chapter One: Leaving Home

The old Disks A–D story has been replaced. Its rescues, time-travel railway, registry, receipts and missing-father plot are not part of this campaign. The full planned story, including the separate final chapter in the god's domain, is in [grand-outline.md](grand-outline.md). Later chapters are unimplemented author knowledge, not facts the party already knows.

- Nell is seventeen, a railway apprentice at Larkspur. Her older brother Tomas, twenty-four, has returned from the capital. They have promised to see the sea together. Nell carries Ansel's oil lantern.
- Larkspur is holding a festival. The empire credits the Benefactor with crystal lighting, plentiful harvests, healing and its engines. The optional festival-lantern interaction records Nell learning the patron's name and emblem (`blessingRead`). The public festival blessing is not proof of the bargain's terms.
- Stationmaster Ansel is Nell's mentor. Iona supplies the station. Orrin has built a coal-fired engine independent of imperial crystal signals.
- Captain Severin arrives to arrest Tomas for stealing a crystal from the palace engine. Ansel intervenes and is fatally wounded. Tomas drops the crystal during the struggle. Its light enters Nell's lantern, briefly stopping movement and sound. Nell hears “Return what I gave.” She cannot identify the speaker.
- Tomas is taken aboard the eastbound prison coach. His last instruction is to find Orrin and use the old branch. Ansel dies in Nell's arms after directing her west. `raidSeen` records the entire scene only when it completes or is skipped. A return visit offers Ansel's cap and Iona's acknowledgment, never a living Ansel.
- Nell defeats two pursuit hounds (`escapeWon`), opening the western road. This is the only solo battle.
- Orrin hears what happened, supports Nell and joins deliberately (`orrinJoined`). His engine can use the Rillbridge branch to reach the eastern pass. No fuse or repair errands gate recruitment or departure.
- An optional workshop encounter (`workshopWon`) rewards the brass lantern reflector. It is not needed to proceed. Neither rewards nor supplies can be collected repeatedly.
- Rillbridge has stopped. Its people, smoke and water are motionless. Nell's lantern allows Orrin to move beside her. The imperial collector is still running at the eastern carriage. The sun emblem matches the crystal's light. `villageSeen` records this arrival.
- Through the collector's signal horn, Severin calls the operation a repayment. The party defeats the collector guard (`collectorDefeated`), then physically opens the casing in the release scene (`villageFreed`). The villagers wake. Tilda has lost three days and her hands hurt; Elspeth cannot lift her register. The party stopped further taking, but has not restored what was already taken.
- Tilda directs them to the eastern pass, where Tomas's coach went. Tilda and Elspeth remain in the village and acknowledge being freed on repeat visits. The north road opens only after the rescue.
- Severin holds the pass. The optional mural shows people entering a hollow sun, their shadows becoming children and old people. Orrin observes that it predates the imperial track; nobody explains the god's realm yet.
- The shelter offers free rest and two bandages once. It does not advance a countdown. The captain uses a blast attack; orange steam outlets discharge after each combat round. Movement, ally damage, cover and previews retain the engine's existing rules.
- Defeating Severin (`captainDefeated`) genuinely opens the crossing. He retreats alive. In the departure scene he says Tomas is at the Iron Coast prison-train exchange and warns that “he” will come for everything given. Nell and Orrin choose to follow in their own engine.
- The player sees an eye open inside a hollow sun beneath the palace, and hears the same voice. Nell and Orrin do not witness this final cutaway. The completed departure sets `chapterOneComplete`, boundary `chapter-two:iron-coast`, and the `departure` journal entry together.

Save version 5 identifies campaign `imperial-debt`. Previous-story saves can be exported but are explicitly rejected for play. The old campaign's content and flags must not be used to infer new events. Cutscenes commit at completion; interruptions replay from the preceding checkpoint, and skipping invokes the same completion callback. Exploration and combat are separate from temporary cutscene actors.
