# Playable canon — the seven-chapter campaign

The old Disks A–D story has been replaced. Its rescues, time-travel railway, registry, receipts and missing-father plot are not part of this campaign. All seven chapters are playable, including both Chapter Six outcomes and the recovered route into the Benefactor’s domain. [grand-outline.md](grand-outline.md) records the complete storyline. The chapter sections below describe knowledge and outcomes at their respective checkpoints.

The allied crews carry the completed anchor regions’ training instructions and fittings aboard. After the chart changes in Chapter Six, their supply counters continue offering Orrin’s counterbalance, Petra’s shield-recovery drill and Celia’s focus calibration. The original specialists remain in their communities; purchases share their existing quest gates, prices and once-only flags. See [the economy rules](../economy.md#permanent-improvements).

The [continuity notes](continuity-notes.md) track the raid witnesses, Rillbridge's missing people and route-dependent knowledge.

- Nell is seventeen, a railway apprentice at Larkspur. Her older brother Tomas, twenty-four, has returned from the capital. They have promised to see the sea together. Nell carries Ansel's oil lantern.
- Larkspur is holding a festival. The empire credits the Benefactor with crystal lighting, plentiful harvests, healing and its engines. The optional festival-lantern interaction records Nell learning the patron's name and emblem (`blessingRead`). The public festival blessing is not proof of the bargain's terms.
- Stationmaster Ansel is Nell's mentor. Iona supplies the station. Orrin has built a coal-fired engine independent of imperial crystal signals.
- Captain Alban arrives to arrest Tomas for stealing a crystal from the imperial conduit. Ansel intervenes and is fatally wounded. Tomas drops the crystal during the struggle. A pale fleck catches in Nell's lantern glass. Its handle hums and feels cold. Neither the scene nor the journal establishes what the crystal can do; no divine voice speaks.
- Tomas sees Alban strike Ansel, then a visible imperial soldier escorts him aboard the eastbound prison coach before Ansel dies. His last instruction is to find Orrin and use the old branch. Ansel dies in Nell's arms after directing her west. `raidSeen` records the entire scene only when it completes or is skipped. A return visit offers Ansel's cap and Iona's acknowledgment, never a living Ansel.
- Nell defeats two pursuit hounds in Larkspur square (`escapeWon`), opening the western road. The battle uses the station backdrop. This is the only solo battle.
- Orrin hears what happened, supports Nell and joins deliberately (`orrinJoined`). His engine can use the Rillbridge branch to reach the eastern pass. No fuse or repair errands gate recruitment or departure.
- An optional workshop encounter (`workshopWon`) rewards the brass lantern reflector. It is not needed to proceed. Neither rewards nor supplies can be collected repeatedly.
- Rillbridge has stopped. Its people, smoke and water are motionless. The imperial collector is still running at the eastern carriage. The lantern hums more strongly here, but the party does not explain why they can move or attribute time powers to the crystal. `villageSeen` records this arrival.
- Through the collector's signal horn, Alban calls the operation a repayment. The party defeats the collector guard (`collectorDefeated`), then physically opens the casing in the release scene (`villageFreed`). The remaining villagers wake. Alban records a charge of three days from every household; this is the collection’s cost, not a three-day calendar jump during the pursuit. Tilda’s hands hurt and Elspeth cannot lift her register. The porter and other residents remain missing; Tilda searches while Elspeth keeps count. Stopping the machine prevents further taking but does not restore those already taken.
- Tilda directs them to the eastern pass, where Tomas's coach went. Tilda and Elspeth remain in the village and acknowledge being freed on repeat visits. The north road opens only after the rescue.
- Alban holds the pass. The optional mural shows harvesters carrying sheaves toward a damaged sun carving. Orrin observes that it predates the imperial track; nobody explains the god's realm yet.
- The shelter offers free rest and two bandages once. It does not advance a countdown. The captain uses a blast attack; orange steam outlets discharge after each combat round. Movement, ally damage, cover and previews retain the engine's existing rules.
- Defeating Alban (`captainDefeated`) genuinely opens the crossing. He retreats alive. In the departure scene he says Tomas is at the Iron Coast prison-train exchange and warns that the exchange is under military guard. Nell and Orrin choose to follow in their own engine.
- The chapter ends with Nell and Orrin leaving together, without a palace cutaway, divine speech or a revealed source for the lantern's hum. The completed departure sets `chapterOneComplete`, boundary `chapter-two:iron-coast`, and the `departure` journal entry together.

Save version 10 identifies campaign `imperial-debt` and stores a shared equipment bag with individual loadouts and the active formation. Only this version is supported; older saves are not migrated. The old campaign's content and flags must not be used to infer new events. Cutscenes commit at completion; interruptions replay from the preceding checkpoint, and skipping invokes the same completion callback. Exploration and combat are separate from temporary cutscene actors.


## Chapter Two — Prison Trains

- Silent dock workers and waiting families populate the exchange; freed prisoners appear beside the opened coach until reaching the ward. Patients, visitors and a supply worker occupy the ward. Cinderbank families wait near the sheds, gather at the medical coaches after the collector fight, and leave the platform when boarding completes. These nameless people add no dialogue or errands, and their appearances follow existing rescue flags.
- The explicit `chapter-two:iron-coast` boundary opens the chapter. All three regional maps load before it is consumed; failure preserves the complete departure. A source checkpoint without the coast art pack offers no Begin Chapter Two button.
- Kit arrives as a registered courier delivering medicine and letters. Alban tears his permit, seizes the satchel and throws it into the barred coach. Petra pulls a hound off Kit’s satchel and asks Alban to honor the permit, but still makes Kit stand back. Nell watches from farther down the platform; Alban does not see her during this encounter.
- At the loading gate Nell returns a piece of Kit’s permit and asks about Tomas. Kit saw Tomas at the transfer window that morning, when the army refused to let him send a letter. Kit agrees to guide Nell and Orrin to recover his deliveries. He is not a resistance operative or a volunteer train robber. `kitJoined` commits only when this conversation completes.
- The signal walk has two crows and a hound; the loading gate has an armored guardian and two hounds. The choice is explained before commitment and saved as `raidPlanned` plus `raidDiversion`. Both battles pay one shared `trainRaidWon` reward and put a courier sight in the bag for manual fitting.
- Petra recognizes Nell’s lantern from Alban’s report and orders her to surrender it. Kit and Orrin move a wagon or loading crane across her route; the party fights the guard machines. The lantern only hums against Nell’s hand. No divine voice speaks.
- Opening the coach (`prisonersFreed`) frees the prisoners and returns Kit’s medicine. A prisoner gives Nell a slip Tomas asked him to keep for his sister: imperial conduit works, Northglass, personal custody by imperial order. Tomas added that the collectors feed the engines and he saw houses go dark. This establishes a practical connection, not the whole divine bargain.
- Orrin finds current medical-train clearance in the dispatch case. Kit knows its capital siding from his delivery rounds. One brief flustered response to Nell’s thanks ends with him returning to work; it has no reciprocal implication or later payoff.
- At the railway ward, Dena receives the prisoners and signs for Kit’s medicine (`wardSeen`). The official hospital refused people without release papers. Kit has a letter for a Cinderbank family whose father is already aboard. Orrin can leave directly for the capital, but Nell deliberately chooses the branch detour to collect the families. Dena travels with the patients as a supporting actor. Free rest and bandages at eight marks remain available. The engine, ward and Cinderbank medical coaches also offer coastal equipment, ordinary bandages and sales of spare gear. Stronger field bandages and focus tonics enter medical shops in Chapter Four.
- The traveling party is Nell, Orrin and Kit. Celia neither appears nor joins; her introduction is deferred to a later chapter. The workshop continues to preview her build. The mechanical three-fighter cap has no story explanation yet.
- At Cinderbank, Alban orders the settlement collected to feed the capital’s engines. Petra objects to abandoning the promised evacuation; he cites the emperor’s superseding order (`settlementSeen`). Orrin opens the casing before the battle. The party holds the gold loading walk while Dena brings families aboard: three clear rounds or defeating all machines completes `coastCollectorWon` and awards a reinforced work coat. Blocked families wait safely; no civilian death clock runs. Checking with Dena completes `evacuationReady` and reunites Kit’s letter recipients. Previously taken days are not restored.
- Petra accepts the patients’ clearance but insists on arresting Nell and recovering the fragment. Alban retreats behind her shield at the far crossing. Petra is the chapter boss, using Shield bash, Taunt and Saber sweep alongside two command crows; Alban is not a combat target. Winning `recaptureWon` disarms Petra and opens the crossing.
- After Petra’s defeat, Alban orders crows to fire on the departing coach doors. Petra steps into their line and refuses. Alban strips her commission; she agrees to report to the capital and stays to countermand the firing order among her soldiers. She witnesses Orrin’s coal engine move the full medical train without a crystal. The prisoners and Cinderbank families escape on that engine and the ward’s coaches. Kit’s deliveries are complete; he chooses to see the passengers safely through to the capital and help Nell find Tomas. Completion commits `chapterTwoComplete`, journal `capitalDeparture` and boundary `chapter-three:capital` together.

The emperor’s first direct scene remains reserved for the capital chapter. Celia does not appear until the Chapter Four harbor and has no connection to Tomas’s transfer. The complete bargain, the conduit and the Benefactor’s separate domain require later discoveries. The Chapter Three rescue must succeed permanently and bring Tomas back alive.

## Chapter Three — The Conduit Works

- `chapter-three:capital` begins Chapter Three only after the tiled `northglass`, `relayhall` and `conduit` maps load. Their map data owns doors, controls, arrivals and scene casts. She cites his firing order at Cinderbank, refuses again and snaps his command rod. She holds the coach doors while the party defeats the custody machines (`capitalConfronted`, `capitalGateWon`). Alban leaves for the palace alive. Completing Petra’s next scene commits `petraJoined`, awards a balanced saber hilt and adds her to the roster. The active formation stays at three; Equipment can swap her in. No diegetic explanation for the cap is given.
- A maintenance chest in the square gives Orrin a baffled pressure nozzle once (`capitalCache`). It is an upgrade over his earlier nozzle and goes to the shared bag.
- In the control gallery, Tomas is holding a valve casing open, keeping the outer alarm off. The engine warden authorizes his execution (`worksSeen`). Defeating it (`worksWardenWon`) stops its shears and rewards Nell’s silver reflector. Opening the restraints completes the rescue (`tomasRescued`). Interrupted scenes replay without duplicating recruitment or rewards.
- Nell and Tomas reunite. Tomas asks whether Ansel survived the wound he witnessed; Nell confirms that he died after Tomas was taken away. He explains that the collectors deliver people’s days to the vessel below and that he stole a piece to expose the operation. His knowledge does not establish what Nell’s lantern can do. He bypasses the execution circuit, then Kit takes him and the gallery fitters to Dena at the medical train. He disappears from the gallery and becomes available by the engine, alive and safe. He is never a combat unit.
- The optional sealed-stores patrol warns of its sentinel and hound before commitment. It rewards Kit’s ratchet sight once (`palaceStoresWon`). Declining does not affect the rescue or main route.
- At the central control table the party chooses its intake approach (`intakePlanned`, boolean `pressureRoute`). The feeds route requires occupying both blue earth plates together to release the regulator’s permanent shield. The pressure route has two crows and marked steam outlets. The regulator is a towering mechanical boss with a three-hex triangular base; the party faces upward toward it. Its announced pressure attacks keep their marked hexes for the round. The sealed route needs two active companions before combat starts. Both routes reach the same console, pay one shared `conduitGuardWon` reward and yield an insulated work coat usable by any companion.
- On reaching the chamber (`conduitSeen`), the party sees a faceless crystal vessel restrained within the engine. The emperor appears in person and demands the fragment. He defends the engines as the capital’s protection; Orrin points to the coal train’s working alternative. The emperor activates the regulator. The Benefactor remains silent and the complete bargain is not explained.
- After the party defeats the regulator, physically closing the feeds commits `extractionStopped` and `emperorMerged` together. The Emperor uses the last charge to teleport from a shuttered transfer chamber to the floating marble capital. He orders the coastal relay closed. Pipes buckle and dust falls as the works shut down. This is his new decision, not proof that he planned the rescue.
- At the south platform, Nell finds Tomas already with the patients. Orrin’s coal engine carries everyone out. The last bridge falls behind the train; a mill wheel in the valley stops. The completion commits `chapterThreeComplete`, journal `brokenDeparture` and `chapter-four:broken-world`. Tomas’s rescue and the earlier evacuations remain permanent.

The four recruited travelers are Nell, Orrin, Kit and Petra; three can fight. Celia has not appeared. Level 6 begins at 520 XP and level 7 at 700 XP. All five equipment rewards go into the bag and remain subject to normal manual equipment and one-copy-per-wearer rules.
