## Knowledge at the Chapter Two departure

Nell and Orrin have read Tomas’s transfer slip, freed the exchange prisoners, stopped the Cinderbank collection and seen the emperor’s order enforced by Alban. Tomas is alive in personal custody at the capital’s palace engine works. They have a current medical-train clearance and are pulling the medical coaches there with Orrin’s coal engine. They still cannot explain the lantern’s resonance or why they could move in Rillbridge.

Kit knows the exchange and capital siding from his delivery rounds. Alban seized his medicine and letters; the rescue allowed him to complete those deliveries. He knows the families from his book and has chosen to see the passengers safely to the capital. Celia has not met the party; none of the Chapter Two events is part of her established knowledge.

Petra knows Nell carries the sought fragment. She objected to abandoning Cinderbank but still fought to arrest Nell, shielding Alban at the crossing. After her defeat she refused fire on the coaches and lost her commission. She saw a coal engine move the passengers without imperial crystals, and stayed to countermand the firing order among her soldiers. She is not a recruit or reserve yet. Alban leaves alive; he was not the Chapter Two boss. Ansel remains dead; Tomas remains captive until Chapter Three.

## Knowledge at the Chapter Three departure

Nell, Orrin, Kit and Petra freed Tomas and stopped the extraction feeds. Tomas explained that the collectors feed people’s days to the restrained vessel. The party saw the emperor enter it rather than accept the shutdown, the capital rise and time fail in the valley. None has heard the Benefactor speak or learned the complete founding bargain or the source beyond the conduit. Nell’s lantern still supplies sensory clues rather than a confirmed time-power explanation.

Tomas is alive with the medical passengers. He knows Ansel was killed by Alban and that Nell used Orrin’s coal engine to find him. His sabotage made the rescue possible; he remains an active supporting person, not a combat unit or a new kidnapping objective. Petra has openly refused an arrest order, broken Alban’s command rod and joined the party at a real cost. Celia has not met anyone in the party yet.

The Emperor is an older human man with silver hair, gray eyes, a short beard, an ivory/navy coat, brass sun circlet and dark mourning ribbon. His first direct appearance is the extraction chamber. He claims the capital depends on the engines, refuses to release collection and chooses merger only after the feeds close. He has not yet offered Nell her old life or been defeated. His family history and final confrontation remain later story material.


## Knowledge at the Chapter Four departure

The four travelers stayed together after the capital escape. Celia joined them while treating ferry crews at Breakwater. They opened the harbor by hand, cleared the sanctuary bridge, disconnected its imperial return line and defeated the warden's local reserve. The coast is moving again. Hammerplain, Dawnwatch and Windmere remain intact distant anchors supporting the palace conduit.

Celia is an older physician who taught a shortened hospice oath. The covered founding pledge revealed the missing price: descendants' days for harvests, healing and light. She acknowledges that the miracles healed people while rejecting that charge. She left an unshortened copy with Dena. An older diagram distinguishes the palace conduit from a source beyond the hollow sun. Nobody has opened the passage or heard the Benefactor speak; the lantern remains unexplained.

Bram is a harbor foreman with a brass work helmet, gray beard, blue sleeves, leather apron and right-hand mallet. His ferry brings the crews to Dena and keeps the refuge supplied. He knows a survey ship at the western airfield. Bram and the hollow-ring Sanctuary Warden use their new vector bodies and matching portraits. The warden is a mechanical hospice keeper obeying an imperial signal, not a divine voice.

Dena keeps all the earlier medical passengers safe and receives the ferry crews. Tomas stays at the refuge coordinating railway messages, alive and never a combatant. He and Nell have taken their promised walk to the sea. Nell chose the anchor counterattack after being offered a chance to stay. Petra thinks some Dawnwatch comrades will listen to her. Alban survives offstage; his resolution remains planned for Chapter Five. Ansel's death remains permanent.

## Chapter Five — Hammerplain

The [four-act plan](chapter-five-plan.md) gives Orrin the Hammerplain engine changeover, Petra the Dawnwatch evacuation and Celia the Windmere hospital shutdown, in the player's chosen order. Kit reconnects their crews through his courier work. Nell faces the Emperor's offer of an old Larkspur morning after two regional victories; the projection does not resurrect Ansel. In the fourth-act finale the party disables Alban’s flagship, then the unseen Benefactor destroys him, his men and the hull. The shared opening, Hammerplain, Dawnwatch and the second-victory Emperor offer are implemented. Windmere is also implemented; the divine intervention, Nell’s illness, the hunters’ attack and the capital departure are now playable in Act IV.

Alda is the older survey captain, blue-coated with gray hair and a pale scarf. She was delivering food when Durn seized her ship; she now flies supplies and chosen anchor landings. Hester is Hammerplain’s casting forewoman, with rolled sleeves, russet headwrap and a wrench. Her crew built its own ordinary engines before the party arrived. Orrin helps her reopen the cooling gate and learns to let another crew run its own work. Hester’s crew keeps Durn alive in the shift office after his attempt to melt their engines fails. Durn wears a green imperial coat and carries his output warrant; he is a local superintendent, separate from Alban.

After Act I, the street is moving and Hammerplain’s crew supplies engines, fitters and a spare drive. Alban’s fleet is visible inland but has not engaged. Kit’s bag-carrying fluster at the airfield remains a brief unresolved joke, with no mutual romance. All five heroes participate in scenes regardless of the combat formation. Tomas keeps the railway talking from the refuge; the god has not spoken and the lantern’s hum is unexplained. Exact current knowledge and checkpoint: [chapter-five-continuation.md](chapter-five-continuation.md).

## Chapter Five — Dawnwatch and the second anchor

Commander Aurel is Petra’s former superior: an older man with cropped gray hair, a square face, a plum command coat and brass shoulder plates. He orders the lower town abandoned for the anchor, fights beside shield troops, and remains alive after surrender. He does not receive an easy pardon; the shelter watch protects him in custody. Petra admits having enforced similar orders herself. She does not regain a commission.

Enna, a watch soldier with short dark hair, a green scarf, teal uniform and shield, remembers Cinderbank’s medical coaches. Her sister is in Dawnwatch’s shelter. She helps Petra open the gate, refuses fire into it and releases the anchor under town control. The watch stays; volunteer pilots send two escort launches, approach charts and recognition signals to Alda. Other soldiers disarm rather than joining the party. Both have their own 80px actors and 128px editable portraits.

The first freed region triggers Alban’s distant fleet sighting. After any two of the three regions, Nell sees the Emperor’s repeating projection of Larkspur in the observation glass. She recognizes Ansel’s habit of turning his chipped cup toward himself, lingers and asks whether he can leave the station. She walks out and tells the companions she almost asked how to reach that day. Kit reads Tomas’s ordinary message. This is no resurrection or demonstrated death reversal: Ansel remains dead, Tomas is at the refuge, the god is silent and the lantern unexplained. The fleet’s inland cordon closes without a timer or blocked landing.

## Chapter Five — Windmere

Director Iven is an older hospital keeper with spectacles, silver hair and beard, a cream coat, gold collar and green ledger. He knows the complete pledge and has hidden the villages’ losses. He helped patients recover and fears losing that work. He releases the mirrored engine when Celia proceeds with ordinary care, then survives to open his records under staff and village supervision. No easy pardon or execution resolves his responsibility.

Sera is the ward nurse, dark-haired with a pinned cap, teal coat and pale apron. She knows the stored equipment and old lift, feels implicated by referrals she signed without the hidden information, and works with Celia to transfer patients before shutdown. She keeps the morning clinic open, sends a medical crew and lends Celia her spare focus. Her actor and portrait use independent female face planes.

Teren is an adult patient in a russet jacket, with short dark hair. His sister is relieved to see him walk again. His treatment was real; he retains the healing after shutdown and continues rehabilitation under ordinary care. All three have distinct native actors and editable 128px portraits. The engine has its own optical silhouette and mechanical portrait. The god stays silent and the lantern remains unexplained.

## Knowledge at the Chapter Five departure

All three anchors are disconnected. The alliance's engines, pilots and medics made the capital crossing possible. The party disabled Alban's flagship; Petra confronted him before an arrest. The Benefactor then destroyed him, his men and the entire hull. He is dead, never a prisoner or recruit, and cannot escape again. Petra wanted him to answer before the people he hurt; the god denied that reckoning. His earlier crimes remain his choices.

The Benefactor spoke as an unseen presence through a seam of light. It called the ships its gift, retaliated for the anchors and demanded the fragment. No one has seen its body. Its glasswings and sun herald attacked Nell's medical hatch and were defeated. The Emperor's threat and the god's separate agency are both established.

Nell's connection to the shard makes her ill even away from the lantern. She is awake at the berth but unavailable for combat. Her roster entry, level and equipment persist. Kit pulled her across the gangway, stays beside her and takes Celia's notes for Sera. Care and competent courier work deepen his attachment without a confession or a resolved romance. Kit usually leads the field party while she recovers; the selected companions remain the player's choice.

Nell has told the others about the Emperor's projection and can still decide to refuse it. Her temptation remains for Chapter Six, alongside the optional recovery route described in [its plan](chapter-six-plan.md). Tomas keeps the railway messages at the refuge. The medical passengers stay safe and Ansel stays dead. The god-domain journey is still separate; its final form remains unseen.


## Chapter Six — Namar

Samira is Namar’s queen: a middle-aged woman with dark hair, warm brown skin, a low bronze diadem and a teal robe. She reads the freed regions’ ledger, chooses to revoke the pact, escapes her own barred palace with the party and operates the original observatory release herself. She acknowledges her palace’s role in buying imperial grain and commits the city’s navigation and barges publicly. She is a supporting actor, not a recruit.

Sahir is the minister: a broad-faced, bearded man with a pale wrapped cap, plum coat and bronze keeper’s token. He fears the Benefactor and believes the Emperor is their only protection. He bars the queen’s court and wakes the observatory guardian; it does not obey him. He survives to answer before Namar’s watch. Fear does not absolve him or grant a pardon.

Amal runs the evening post: a young adult woman with a cream headscarf, dark side braid, blue coat and leather postal bag. She maintains the railway correspondence, forwards Kit’s letters and opens the postal stair after the palace seizure. She has routes, colleagues and a city to care for beyond the party’s quest. All three have independent native vector actors and portrait originals; the women use the female face construction.

The companions have seen the capital barrier, learned where the two pact seals are held, and heard Sera’s river referral. After Namar only the western seal is open. Nell receives the news aboard, still ill, and Kit’s concern remains practical and unresolved. The northern expedition and river treatment are upcoming gameplay, not completed offscreen events.
