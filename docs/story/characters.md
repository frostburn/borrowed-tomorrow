## Companions and recruitment

These three companions are available in the Vite workshop so their combat roles can be tested. Kit and Celia now join in Chapter Two. Petra appears in that chapter and remains a planned Chapter Three recruit. None belongs to the Chapter One party. Kit has new matching art; Celia and Petra use previously unused asset pairs assigned to their reboot identities. The traveling cast may grow, but the active combat party has at most three members; reserves keep their own builds and equipment.

## Knowledge at the Chapter Two departure

Nell and Orrin have read Tomas’s transfer slip, freed the exchange prisoners, stopped the Cinderbank collection and seen the emperor’s order enforced by Alban. Tomas is alive in personal custody at the capital’s palace engine works. They have a current medical-train clearance and are pulling the medical coaches there with Orrin’s coal engine. They still cannot explain the lantern’s resonance or why they could move in Rillbridge.

Kit knows the exchange and capital sidings, the rescued prisoners and their waiting families. His priorities extend beyond Nell. He has joined the journey, supplied the route and kept a list of the rescued people. Celia knows Tomas from treating his wrist and knows the capital’s medical siding. She saw the official hospital refuse prisoners and the army abandon Cinderbank. She has not learned the full bargain or lost all faith after a single conversation.

Petra knows Nell carries the sought fragment, objected to Alban’s abandonment order, cleared the platform and changed the points for the evacuation. She stayed behind to bring her soldiers off the west gate. She is not a recruit, a reserve or an enemy secretly controlling the escape. Alban survives both defeats. Ansel remains dead; Tomas remains captive until Chapter Three.
