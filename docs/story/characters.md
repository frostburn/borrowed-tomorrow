# Characters and knowledge

| Character | Motive / voice | Knows after Disk A |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nell | Practical, observant, willing to help before understanding everything. Station work gives her a vocabulary of parcels, platforms, and passengers. Let her be funny without making every line a quip. | The train is real; Mira is displaced; the timetable is a rescue route with restrictive rules; Rillbridge's flood is still ahead. |
| Orrin | Mechanic; distrusts explanations that fail a physical inspection. Warmth appears through useful actions. His father took him to the signal yard. | Fresh maintenance exists on the old railway. If optional salvage was completed, he recognizes his father's handwriting. He does not yet know why his father's work survives. |
| Mira | Wants her father and suitcase. Notices concrete details adults miss. Courage is compatible with being frightened. | Recognizes her home beyond the tunnel. Adults say her ticket is old; she has no explanation for the time displacement. |
| Ansel | Stationmaster who puts passengers ahead of ministry orders. | The ministry wants the train impounded. May have received Nell's promise to bring the passengers home. |
| Iona | Supply clerk; dry humor, practical kindness. | Has heard enough to offer the party rest. Not secretly all-knowing. |
| Inspector | Enforces the passenger registry literally; repeats orders when challenged. | Knows Mira's registration and that Nell and Orrin do not belong to this service. Identity and degree of agency remain undisclosed. |
| Ada Fenwick | Offscreen author of the return railway. A rescuer whose maintenance system outlived its original purpose. | Private author knowledge only; no character in Disk A names her. |

Flags: `promisedHome` and `askedForTruth` are mutually exclusive answers to Ansel; the first choice stays fixed on later visits. Older saves containing both answers can resolve the ambiguity on their next visit. Preserve the chosen answer for later dialogue. `scrapCleared` governs whether Orrin knows about his father's envelope. Party recruitment uses `orrinJoined` and a matching party record.
