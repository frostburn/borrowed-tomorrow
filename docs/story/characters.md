# Characters and knowledge — Chapter One

| Character | Motive and voice | Knowledge at the saved departure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nell | Seventeen; she/her. Direct, impatient to leave home, protective of Tomas. Grief sometimes interrupts her practical determination. | Saw the arrest, Ansel's death, the stopped village and Alban's defeat. Knows Tomas is at the Iron Coast. Heard the repayment claim and felt the lantern's hum and cold handle. Does not know what the crystal can do, why they could move in Rillbridge, the full bargain or the emperor's plan. |
| Orrin | Young mechanic; tests explanations with his hands, offers support before explanations, makes occasional dry jokes. | Knows what Nell told him about the raid and crystal. Saw the collector taking light, the villagers wake and Alban retreat. His coal engine works without imperial crystals. Does not have a missing-father subplot. |
| Tomas | Nell's brother, twenty-four; affectionate, worried, trying to protect her. | Knows why he stole the palace crystal. What he discovered will be earned in the rescue chapters. Captive at the Iron Coast exchange; not a combatant or a follower. |
| Stationmaster Ansel | Nell's mentor; practical, gently teasing, willing to oppose armed authority for his people. | Dies during the raid. Only his cap and others' memories remain available. |
| Iona | Supply clerk, dry humor and direct kindness. | Witnessed the aftermath; stays with Ansel and keeps the station open. Knows Nell is pursuing Tomas. |
| Captain Alban | Human imperial officer; precise, coercive, convinced ordinary people owe the empire obedience for its protection. | Took Tomas, oversaw the Rillbridge repayment and tried to recover Nell's fragment. Defeated at the pass and withdrew. Knows more about the collection than he tells the party. |
| Tilda | Rillbridge baker; concerned first with her hands, bread and neighbors. | Knows the army brought the collector, Nell and Orrin freed the village, three days passed, and the coach went east. |
| Elspeth | Retired teacher using a wheelchair; precise, independent, still counting people. | Recognizes her new weakness, helps organize the freed village and tells Nell to go. |
| The Benefactor | Worshipped as the empire's patron; its actual nature belongs to the later author plan. | Makes no direct appearance and never speaks in Chapter One. The crystal's powers and origin remain uncertain. |

Tomas and Alban have new matching portraits and actors. Ansel and Iona retain the original `master` and `clerk` art; these identities are not reused. Nell and Orrin retain their existing directional movement frames. Tilda and Elspeth retain their own matching art. Unseen speakers and narration have no portrait.


## Planned companions — workshop preview

These three companions are available in the Vite workshop so their combat roles can be tested. Their recruitment and relationship scenes belong to later chapters; none has joined the playable Chapter One party. Kit has new matching art; Celia and Petra use previously unused asset pairs assigned to their reboot identities. The traveling cast may grow, but the active combat party has at most three members; reserves keep their own builds and equipment.

| Companion | Introduction and recruitment plan | Personality and relationships | Combat role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kit | A resistance courier at the Iron Coast exchange. Joins in Chapter Two while guiding the prison-train raid. His own priority is getting the prisoners and their families onto a safe route. | A seventeen-year-old boy, Nell’s peer. Short tousled brown hair, a dusty blue courier jacket, ochre scarf and satchel. Quick with routes and improvised solutions; occasionally loses his words when Nell thanks him, then gets back to the task. His flustered reactions are one-sided hints of puppy love; Nell need not notice. | Fast skirmisher with a repeating crossbow. Quick shot, armor-piercing bolts, a distracting feint and a volley. |
| Celia | A physician educated in the Benefactor’s hospitals. Joins the Chapter Two rescue because injured prisoners need care and the official hospitals refuse them. | Older, precise, patient with frightened people and impatient with preventable harm. Her medical knowledge and concern for patients survive her loss of faith in the institution. She does not know the full divine bargain on recruitment. | Healing and support: Mend, focus restoration, Beacon and an area barrier. |
| Petra | An imperial knight pursuing the fragment in Chapter Two. Helps the escape after witnessing the army abandon a settlement; becomes a permanent companion in Chapter Three after refusing an order to hand the escapees back. | Disciplined, observant, slow to make excuses for herself. Her change of allegiance requires action and a cost; one conversation does not settle her loyalties. Her former commander remains a later opponent. | Armored vanguard. Shield bash pushes enemies, Cover protects an ally, Saber sweep clears adjacent hexes, and Crushing blow pierces armor. |

Keep Kit’s flustered moments brief, innocent and occasional. He has work, friends and obligations beyond Nell. Leave the hints unresolved throughout the story: no mutual-romance arc, confession, kiss, romance quest or couple ending. Nell’s ordinary friendliness does not imply reciprocation. Let the joke pass without humiliating him or explaining it to the player.
