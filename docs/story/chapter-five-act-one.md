# Chapter Five — Act I implementation

This slice implements the shared airfield opening and Hammerplain. Dawnwatch and Windmere now ship as independent acts. Neither is completed or awarded by finishing Hammerplain. The flagship remains a separate dev preview.

The version-10 `chapter-five:anchors` departure leads to the western airfield without changing the recruited roster, chosen formation, HP/focus, resources or gear. Captain Alda's survey crew is loading supplies when Superintendent Durn orders their seizure. Clear the guards, board the working ship, and choose the Hammerplain landing from its flight chart. The ship provides supplies, Equipment access and short conversations throughout the act.

The survey ship remains visibly moored at Hammerplain’s western landing, with a boarding gangway back to its deck. At Hammerplain, casting forewoman Hester is trying to reopen the cooling race while the foundry powers an anchor holding the workers' streets. Choose the freight lift (ranged crows) or the shallow race walk (hounds), then physically open the eastern cooling wheel. A guarded safety store offers an optional heat-resistant coat. A crew rest bench on the lower gantry offers free rest for the entire roster and bandages before the boss. It saves normally without advancing story flags. At the anchor gantry, Durn sends a mobile furnace carrier to burn the independent engines. Announced furnace lanes and temporary hot-metal trails make its movement matter. It cannot be pushed through other units or leave the grid. Orrin warns about the ladle and hot tracks in ordinary language; precise damage and round timing stay in combat UI.

After the carrier falls, release the anchor with the workers. Hester starts an ordinary engine herself; the streets resume and the foundry contributes engines, propulsion fittings and repair crews. Orrin's new lance fitting enters the bag for manual fitting. On returning to the ship after the first regional victory, Alban's distant fleet appears on the horizon. If Dawnwatch or Windmere was already completed, its sighting stays recorded and the observation-cabin offer becomes available. No countdown starts. Hammerplain stays open for supplies, optional loot and changed return dialogue. Dawnwatch and Windmere keep their actual independent progress, and Chapter Five is not declared complete.

All five exploration maps and five arenas use editable Tiled layouts with whole native SVG scenery. Named cast: Alda, Hester, Durn and the Furnace carrier, each with its own actor and portrait. The accepted vector party and flagship art are preserved. The Vite-only canonical Chapter Five start uses a fresh authored Chapter Four completion at 1225 XP. Exact ending flags and remaining acts are recorded in `chapter-five-continuation.md`; the Benefactor stays silent and Tomas stays safe.
