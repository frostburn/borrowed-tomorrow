# Chapter Six — The Emperor's Last Day

Author plan; not yet playable. Begin from `chapter-six:capital-approach` with Alban and his men dead, all three anchors disconnected, the divine hunters defeated and Nell ill aboard Alda's ship. The player controls the remaining companions, keeping actual formation, equipment and supplies. The Benefactor is an established enemy whose body has not been seen.

## Main route

1. **The outer mooring.** Establish a safe berth, messages to the freed regions and a route into the capital. Kit delivers Celia's notes to the railway courier at the landing. Sera's reply opens the optional recovery quest immediately; the capital route is available independently. Nell is awake enough for brief conversations and has her own wishes about continuing the rescue.
2. **The remembered city.** The Emperor has preserved familiar streets and personal memories while living inhabitants repeat work they cannot finish. Rescue identifiable residents and establish an ordinary evacuation route. Orrin and Petra can lead scenes when Kit/Celia are in reserve. Divine hunters now appear alongside the Emperor's remaining defenses, making their competing aims visible.
3. **The last offer.** The Emperor repeats his promise of the Larkspur morning. Nell may answer from the ship's signal link if still ill, or in person if recovered. She refuses the enclosed, repeating day; neither version resurrects Ansel. The party has practical reasons and allies for dismantling the vessel, beyond saving Nell.
4. **The imperial vessel.** A full upward-facing Emperor boss with a readable sequence of support removal. Every legal formation can finish the main path without Nell. The mortal conduit breaks and the capital is brought down safely with the regional crews' help. The Emperor is decisively defeated; his victims are not blamed for trusting real healing.
5. **The final departure.** The Benefactor widens collection beyond imperial territory. This is escalation of the threat seen at the flagship, not a new villain reveal. The broken vessel and prior diagrams reveal the route through the hollow sun. Give the player time to revisit allies, finish Nell's recovery and deliberately depart for Chapter Seven.

## Optional quest — Kit's delivery

**Purpose:** restore Nell to combat and deepen the two characters' relationship through care, work and mutual trust. It is optional from the outset and is never a concealed prerequisite for defeating the Emperor or finishing the game.

Sera compares Celia's notes with the old open-return treatment records. They suggest that the shard keeps drawing Nell into the god's pulse even away from the lantern. Windmere's existing isolated treatment room and ordinary equipment can interrupt the harmful resonance. Its earlier safe shutdown and staff expertise make this possible; do not invent a second anchor, bargain, hereditary cure or rare-herb shopping list.

Kit knows which courier landings are still passable. Play one connected delivery/rescue route: carry Sera's reply and treatment instructions through a landing occupied by glasswings, restore the courier passage and escort the already-equipped medical crew aboard. Give the hunters a focused encounter with line and wing attacks, rather than repeating the flagship. Nell never accompanies combat while ill. There is no fever timer or offscreen penalty for pursuing the capital first.

Celia and Sera perform the treatment together, with the shard screened in an existing isolated housing. The crystal survives for the final crossing; treatment breaks the harmful response in Nell rather than destroying its story function. Let Nell choose to resume traveling. Kit hands over the completed delivery slip; she notices that he stayed, and he has trouble answering a simple thank-you. No confession, kiss, mutual romance or couple ending follows.

Completion sets `nellRecovered`, restores her HP/focus and makes her available in Equipment. It leaves `nellIll` as a historical flag, preserves all gear/resources and does not displace an active companion automatically. Nell shares earned experience and rejoins at the party's current level. Use alternate scene staging after recovery without branching the whole campaign.

## If the player leaves it unfinished

Nell stays under medical care aboard the ship; the other four remain available in formations of up to three. Her voice and decisions still matter. The recovery quest remains accessible after the Emperor until the clearly marked final departure. Do not remove Kit or make his loyalty depend on accepting it.

In Chapter Seven, the allies keep Nell safe at the crossing while her companions carry the fragment and open the route. If recovered, she can travel and fight normally. A signal connection lets her refuse the offered morning in either version; no confrontation requires her combat unit. The companions can return the fragment and expose the heart. Permanently defeating the Benefactor ends the harmful pulse for an unrecovered Nell too. She survives in both paths; the reward for doing the quest is her earlier recovery, return to play and the personal scenes, not permission to obtain a hopeful ending.

The god's complete body and final transformations are first revealed in its own domain. There is no antagonist behind it. Ansel and Alban are not resurrected, Tomas is not kidnapped again, and the party-size cap remains a game rule.
