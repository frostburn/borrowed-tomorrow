# Chapter Six — The Emperor's Last Day

Author plan; not yet playable. Begin from `chapter-six:capital-approach` with Alban and his men dead, all three anchors disconnected, the divine hunters defeated and Nell ill aboard Alda's ship. The player controls the remaining companions, keeping actual formation, equipment and supplies. The Benefactor is an established enemy whose body has not been seen.

## Approved chapter structure

The party tries to reach the Emperor, but he seals the capital before they can enter. Two seals must be broken at allied capitals in a desert kingdom and an icy northern kingdom. Both destinations can be completed in either order. A jungle route to cure Nell is optional and available from the shared opening. Completing both seals opens the imperial capital; curing Nell is never a prerequisite.

The two seals belong to an old defensive pact. The foreign rulers once entrusted the Emperor with protecting their capitals; he has invoked the pact to close his own. Reach each seal's original seat of authority and win the local support needed to break it. These are foreign courts with their own interests and responsibility for supporting the empire. Their people continue their ordinary lives; the journeys expose the wider consequences of the alliance.

Keep the seven-chapter outline: the two kingdoms, optional jungle and Emperor climax belong to Chapter Six. The deliberate departure into the god's domain remains Chapter Seven. This document records an approved author plan, not implemented scenes, map IDs or new save checkpoints.

## Shared opening — the sealed capital

Alda reaches the outer mooring and the landing bridge extends. The Emperor seals the capital before anyone can cross. People can still be seen moving inside the barrier. He speaks briefly, intending to preserve his city while the rest of the world answers to the Benefactor.

Establish the ship as a safe berth and open the two foreign destinations together. Kit delivers Celia's notes; Sera's reply identifies a medical tradition outside the Benefactor's institutions and opens the jungle lead. Nell remains awake for brief conversations and decisions while the other companions travel. No fever timer, offscreen penalty or forced regional order applies. Preserve actual formation, gear and resources, with Nell unavailable until recovered.

## Desert capital — the royal observatory

**Arrival:** evening markets, shaded waterways, rooftop gardens and a welcoming palace. Kit finds a working postal exchange; Orrin becomes interested in unfamiliar engineering. Leave room for ordinary life and occasional humor.

**Conflict:** the ruler is willing to withdraw support after hearing what happened to Alban. The court is divided. An influential minister believes the Emperor remains their protection against the Benefactor. When the ruler attempts to revoke the pact, the minister occupies the old royal observatory and awakens its guardian.

**Route and climax:** a palace escape leads to an ascent through the observatory above the city. A colossal sun serpent coils around the seal's bronze apparatus. Changing coils expose different targets; glass left by its breath creates reflected attack lanes. Develop the exact tactical sequence during implementation and keep attack shapes readable.

**Payoff:** the ruler breaks the seal publicly. The kingdom has made a consequential choice, and later scenes acknowledge its commitment. Its support helps the capital approach and evacuation. Use original neutral-interval music in 31-EDO throughout the region; the score notation and pitch handling are specified in `docs/music-direction.md`.

## Northern capital — the glacier sanctuary

**Arrival:** a city around a deep blue harbor, steep roofs, covered bridges and great bells that guide ships through snow. Its ruler has already chosen to abandon the pact. Divine hunters have cut off the mountain sanctuary holding its seal. The party joins preparations for an expedition that local people already intend to undertake.

**Route and discovery:** travel through abandoned watch stations and a glacier containing wrecks from an earlier imperial campaign. Those wrecks expose a costly betrayal hidden by the alliance's celebrated history. Let identifiable expedition members respond to what their kingdom had supported.

**Climax:** a winter dragon occupies the sanctuary, its wings spanning the battlefield. It breaks ice to change the stable shelves and telegraphs inhalations that pull fighters toward its jaws. A northern expedition leader helps reach the seal while the party holds it off. No specific companion must be active to win.

**Payoff:** break the second seal when this is the later route, or leave the other capital available when visited first. The northern kingdom contributes ships and navigators. Its main music uses Orwell[9]; Myna[7] is an optional palette for the glacier's dangerous ruins. Both use the existing 31-EDO tuning.

## Optional jungle quest — Nell's recovery

**Purpose:** restore Nell to combat and deepen Kit's care through practical work, trust and a small personal scene. This replaces the earlier plan to cure her entirely through Windmere's isolated room. Sera and Celia remain the medical link to the new region.

Sera identifies a river settlement experienced in treating people harmed by prolonged crystal exposure. Celia recognizes a possible treatment in its records. Follow a river route to a treatment sanctuary threatened by the Benefactor's creatures. Kit helps bring the right people together and get them safely aboard. Give the settlement its own work and concerns; do not turn the route into a rare-herb shopping list or a bargain demanding Kit's sacrifice. The precise encounters and local names remain for implementation.

Nell stays under care while the others travel. The local healers and Celia interrupt the harmful connection without destroying the shard needed for the final crossing. Her recovery is earned through this optional expedition, not by ordinary rest. The region uses Slendric[5] for the river, village and sanctuary, expanding to Slendric[6] for deeper forest and combat.

After treatment, Kit has carefully prepared everything Nell might need, but becomes flustered when she simply asks him to sit with her. Let affection appear through the care itself. No confession, kiss, mutual romance or couple ending follows. Nell chooses when she is ready to travel again.

Completion sets `nellRecovered`, restores her HP/focus and makes her available in Equipment. It leaves `nellIll` as a historical flag, preserves all gear/resources and does not displace an active companion automatically. Nell shares earned experience and rejoins at the party's current level. Stage later scenes for either recovery state without branching the whole campaign. Leave this quest available after the Emperor until the clearly marked final departure.

## Return to the capital and the Emperor

1. **Both seals broken.** The landing bridge finally reaches the capital. The foreign allies contribute the approach, evacuation and medical support. The Emperor has used the party's absence to complete his preserved city; show whom his promised paradise excludes.
2. **The remembered city.** Familiar places have become fragments of his memories while living inhabitants repeat work they cannot finish. Rescue identifiable residents and establish an ordinary evacuation route. Orrin, Kit, Celia and Petra can lead scenes in any legal formation. Divine hunters appear alongside imperial defenses, making their competing aims visible.
3. **The last offer.** The Emperor repeats his promise of the Larkspur morning. Nell may answer from the ship's signal link if still ill, or in person if recovered. She refuses the enclosed, repeating day; neither version resurrects Ansel. The party has practical reasons and allies for dismantling the vessel beyond saving Nell.
4. **The imperial vessel.** A full upward-facing Emperor boss with a readable sequence of support removal. Every legal formation can finish without Nell. The mortal conduit breaks and the regional crews help bring the capital down safely. The Emperor is decisively defeated; his victims are not blamed for trusting real healing. Retain the fragment for Chapter Seven.
5. **The final departure.** The Benefactor widens collection beyond imperial territory, escalating its established threat. The broken vessel and earlier diagrams reveal the route through the hollow sun. Revisit allies, finish optional business and deliberately depart for Chapter Seven. Its domain uses the overtone-derived 31-EDO scale and its inversion documented in `docs/music-direction.md`.

## If the player leaves it unfinished

Nell stays under medical care aboard the ship; the other four remain available in formations of up to three. Her voice and decisions still matter. The recovery quest remains accessible after the Emperor until the clearly marked final departure. Do not remove Kit or make his loyalty depend on accepting it.

In Chapter Seven, the allies keep Nell safe at the crossing while her companions carry the fragment and open the route. If recovered, she can travel and fight normally. A signal connection lets her refuse the offered morning in either version; no confrontation requires her combat unit. The companions can return the fragment and expose the heart. Permanently defeating the Benefactor ends the harmful pulse for an unrecovered Nell too. She survives in both paths; the reward for doing the quest is her earlier recovery, return to play and the personal scenes, not permission to obtain a hopeful ending.

The god's complete body and final transformations are first revealed in its own domain. There is no antagonist behind it. Ansel and Alban are not resurrected, Tomas is not kidnapped again, and the party-size cap remains a game rule.
