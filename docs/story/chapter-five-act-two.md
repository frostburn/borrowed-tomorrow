# Chapter Five Act II — Dawnwatch: orders worth refusing

Playable alongside Hammerplain and Windmere, in any order. The flight chart preserves each unfinished region. Alban's campaign finale remains Act IV.

## Route

Land below Dawnwatch's shelter. Commander Aurel, Petra's former superior, orders its residents abandoned to defend the anchor. Enna served at the medical coaches; other soldiers know only the official account. Petra opens the locking lever with Enna rather than winning the garrison over with a speech. Kit and Celia bring bedding, water and the residents into the shelter. The gate and families change position permanently on completion; interruption replays the uncommitted scene. No escort timer or boarding quota.

Climb the eastern barracks stair. Clear the western mortar or eastern repeater first. The opposite position's spotter announces fixed-round support: a seven-hex mortar blast or a straight repeater lane. Defeating that spotter stops its fire, while the other position still needs clearing. The second battle has no cross-terrace support. Both positions are required before the northern signal-wall stair opens. An officers' field coat is in a side locker after both fights.

Aurel confronts Petra with her own enforcement of similar orders. She admits she sealed doors and never went back to count the cost. Enna refuses an order to clear the shelter by force; Aurel fights with the soldiers still following him. Before his main attack he orders living shield troops to advance one legal hex and brace for six guard. Taunt interrupts the order; his ordinary attacks still follow Taunt and Dazzle. He is a human formation boss, not a giant mechanical encounter.

After defeat he orders reserve fire into the lower gate. The signal watch refuses; others disarm. Petra offers him the shelter's water, bed and protection. Enna turns the anchor wheel and keeps control of the town lamps. Return to the shelter to finish the act. Petra gains no commission. The town sends volunteer pilots, two escort launches, approach charts and recognition signals to Alda; the remaining watch stays with the residents.

## Shared ship scenes

`fleetSighted` follows whichever first region completes. Its dialogue uses that region's contribution. Existing Hammerplain saves keep the same flags and resources.

After any two of the three regional anchors, the objective and Alda point to light in the aft observation cabin. The player enters it. An imperial projection shows a Larkspur morning: Ansel turns the chipped side of his cup toward himself, then repeats the gesture. The Emperor offers Nell a place in the day in exchange for the fragment. She lingers, asks whether Ansel can leave, then walks out. This is a repeating projection, not a resurrection or proof that death can be undone. Ansel remains dead and Tomas remains at the refuge.

Nell tells the others on deck that she almost asked how to get there. Kit reads Tomas's ordinary railway message. The fleet closes its inland formation visually after this scene, without a countdown or closed regional landing. The observation cabin is empty on return. This develops Chapter Six; it does not defeat the Emperor or start the god's domain.
