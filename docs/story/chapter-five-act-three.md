# Chapter Five Act III — Windmere

Windmere is available immediately after the shared flight, alongside Hammerplain and Dawnwatch. All three regional acts accept the actual campaign formation and resources and can be left unfinished. The flagship finale remains Act IV.

## Playable route

1. Choose **Windmere · the lakeside hospital** at the flight chart. Teren walks on a genuinely healed leg. Celia recognizes the treatment; Director Iven admits he knew the pledge's full cost and concealed it from staff and families.
2. Take the eastern walkway into the **lower dispensary**. Sera shows the ordinary beds and breathing bellows stored when the mirrored ward opened. Railway volunteers bring medicine through Dena and Bram's existing network.
3. Clear the **water-lift brake**, then physically turn the eastern wheel. Staff raise the stored ward equipment together. There is no parts hunt.
4. Return to the hospital's **bypass valve**. Staff settle the patients on ordinary support before isolating their return. The transfer is an animated scene, with no timer or patient-death counter.
5. Follow Iven to the **treatment gallery**. He refuses to abandon the anchor's healing and releases the mirrored engine. Clear the gallery bridge, then stop the engine.
6. Work the **support-release controls** above the lens: close treatment supply before opening the upward contact. The records corroborate the hospice diagram's source beyond the hollow sun, but supply no route into it.
7. Return to the ward. Celia stays for the first ordinary night shift. Teren retains his healing and continues rehabilitation; other patients need continuing care. Sera sends medical crews and their tested release sequence to the ship. Iven opens the full ledger to staff and village representatives under supervision.

## Mirrored ward engine

The native 256 × 240 optical engine faces the party from a fixed triangle of three adjacent contacts. Its two 70-HP side projections are anchored, passive targets: they block space, but receive no turns and never retaliate. Two staggered Slag crows screen the engine and attack normally; stopping the core also shuts down this screen.

Each round lights one surviving projection and fixes a reflected fan warning. Breaking that projection interrupts the current beam and opens the core for the rest of that round. Breaking the unlit projection removes a future source but leaves the current beam intact. At the next announcement a surviving projection reseals the core. Once both are broken, the core stays exposed and announces a weaker direct crossbeam through a living hero’s position. It re-sights at the next round announcement; moving never changes the cells already marked. Defeating the core shuts down all remaining projections and ends the fight.

Areas hit each whole unit once. Taunt redirects a live beam, but cannot revive an interrupted one; Dazzle, guard and defense retain their ordinary effects. The presentation captures the fired source before round rollover, so the impact travels through the mirror that actually fired. Native alpha picking targets legal body contacts without redirecting valid grid areas. This machine has an optical firing flash, no breathing animation.
