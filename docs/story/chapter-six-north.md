# Chapter Six — Skeld and the glacier sanctuary

Implementation scope: the northern pact-seal expedition in either order relative to Namar, with Nell ill or recovered. Iria stays available before, during and after the expedition. The Emperor's full city and climax remain later work; both released seals must visibly open the capital and permit a first landing, without granting Chapter Six completion.

## People and route

- **Skeld / the bell harbor.** Steep roofs and covered crossings surround deep blue water. King Edric has already chosen to leave the pact; divine hunters have cut off the mountain sanctuary. Signe leads an expedition the local crews were preparing before the party arrived. Egil, an older harbor pilot, knows the glacier route and the old fleet's names. People are tending boats, supplies and the harbor bells. Establish their work before asking for help.
- **Frostwatch.** Clear the imperial signal post and physically reopen the covered mountain passage. The party earns a warm travel coat. The checkpoint has shelter and ordinary supplies; there is no timed escort or mountaineering shopping list.
- **The glacier wrecks.** Follow the broken relief fleet through exposed blue ice. Its surviving orders show that the empire cut the refugee vessels loose to keep the pact's protected harbor clear. The kingdom's memorial calls this a rescue. Egil recognizes his father's vessel; Signe must carry the actual names and orders home. The discovery is on the main route, not a collectible hunt. Divine hunters contest the upper ascent. An optional stores patrol grants equipment, with a different local musical palette.
- **The winter sanctuary.** Rest at the expedition shelter, then face a great winged winter dragon on the ice walk in front of the sanctuary gate. Its body occupies multiple contacts but area attacks hit it once. Inhalations pull threatened fighters toward its jaws where movement is legal; ice-shattering attacks turn fixed shelves into broken floes. Broken ice is dangerous but passable, so no formation can be stranded or lose to an unexplained fall. Announcements are fixed for the round; Taunt, three-attack Dazzle, guard and reduced motion remain supported.
- **The northern release.** Defeating the dragon alone does not release the pact. Signe operates the sanctuary's release under Edric's commission and breaks the northern seal. Back at the harbor, Edric opens the expedition's records publicly and commits ships and navigators. Leave the town, supplies and return route usable.

The northern seal may be the first or the second. Namar, the ship and Iria acknowledge the actual seal combination. If both are released, allied crews repair the landing bridge and the party reaches a small royal-marble capital quay. Show the Emperor's preserved city beginning to repeat ordinary work, keeping its full confrontation for the next section. Do not invent a third seal or another story blockade to justify that implementation boundary.
