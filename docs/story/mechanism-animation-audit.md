# Story mechanism animation audit

Follow-up to #224. This pass traces authored physical actions to their rendered
props and saved visibility flags. Dialogue, choices, rewards and checkpoint IDs
stay unchanged. The release/opening flag is still written only by the original
scene completion or Skip callback.

| `four-gate` | Gate and ferry switch only after dialogue. | Wheel turns, two leaves fold on their hinges, ferry rounds the quay to the lower mooring. Stone piers stay fixed. | Existing `harborOpened` gate/ferry layers. |

The existing Iria boat sequence now physically raises its channel chain before
it clears; previously the chain only faded. Existing survey boarding/takeoff,
shelter bar and residents, hospital platform/patients, pact barrier, Emperor
transfer, capital release and Benefactor transformations already have dedicated
players and retain them.

Native pose sheets are generated by `assets/vector/story_mechanisms.py` from
the shipped SVG geometry. No raster assets or new runtime dependencies are
introduced. `npm run art:mechanisms` is repeatable; Chapter Seven's exporter
also runs it after its map export.

The browser checks inspect visible output pixels over the actual moving prop,
with the cursor parked, on desktop and fractional-DPR phone layouts. They also
check reduced-motion stability, settlement on the next line, deferred progress
commits, resource preservation, and interrupted reload/skip cleanup.
