# Continuity pass

These corrections follow the events and route conditions in the playable campaign. Existing scene IDs, choices, flags, rewards, saves and chapter boundaries remain intact.

| Point | Correction |
| --- | --- |
| Tomas and Ansel | Tomas sees Alban wound Ansel, then is taken away before Ansel dies. At the Chapter Three reunion he asks whether Ansel survived. Nell confirms the outcome; she does not tell him who attacked Ansel as though he had missed the raid. Alban remains alive until Chapter Five. |
| Rillbridge's three days | The collector records three days from each household as its charge. There is no unexplained Monday-to-Thursday journey while Nell pursues the coach. Tilda's pain and Elspeth's weakness still show the cost; the crystal's protective effects remain unexplained. |
| Rillbridge's missing people | Breaking the local collector frees the remaining residents. The porter and other people already taken are still missing. Elspeth keeps the register and Tilda searches, rather than declaring everyone accounted for. The living Rillbridge captives in Chapter Seven fill this established gap. Their return does not resurrect Ansel or undo the earlier rescue. |
| The lantern at Rillbridge | Nell answers Orrin's question about the louder hum with the vibration she feels through the handle. The early crystal remains a sensory mystery. |
| Automatic battle aftermaths | Victory panels and persistent journals describe the newly exposed controls, captives or charts. They no longer ask the player to activate the follow-up that starts automatically on continuing. This also covers the later anchor releases, capital procession, and flagship/berth scenes. Actual manual steps such as the prison coach, cooling wheel and Frostwatch gate keep their directions. |
| The hollow sun | An empty ring with twelve broad, angled rays recurs on collectors, the old mural, imperial inlays and breastplate, the capital reveal and the domain. The old raster design supplies one missing upper-left wedge and several smaller closed rim fractures, shared by the emblems and both god halos. The sun form keeps its suspended shard; early emblems do not reveal that shard or the god's body. Static domain forms now come from the current battle atlases. |
| The untreated warning | Celia relies on the pulse and failed attempts to move the shard that she has observed herself. She does not cite Ines's treatment notes on a route that may never have reached Iria. The warning, treatment option and bad-ending commitment stay the same. |
| The remembered Tomas | The Emperor's seaside image no longer recalls the real Chapter Four ferry visit. It is the promised holiday Nell is tempted to accept; the real Tomas is still at the refuge. |
| Alda's first evacuees | Before `takenPeopleFreed`, Alda is holding the party's mooring. Only after the first rescue does she report people crossing. The same talk remains appropriate on a return visit. |
| Enna's charts | After `dawnwatchComplete`, she acknowledges that the pilots have taken the charts to Alda. A return to the signal wall does not ask the party to arrange the same volunteers again. |
| The ill berth | Kit's bell demonstration uses Nell's bed frame, matching the berth model. |
| The capital barrier | Petra identifies the cart seen when the Emperor sealed the landing, distinguishing the descending barrier from its later removal. |
| The last pulse check | Celia checks Nell at the sun throne. The hospice patient left with the porter earlier and is not present for the final battle. |

The main canon/overview now identify all seven chapters as implemented. Their checkpoint sections still limit each person's knowledge to that point in the story. Earlier chapter handoffs should not borrow discoveries, deaths or restored captives from later chapters.
