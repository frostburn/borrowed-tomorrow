# Chapter Seven — The God Who Lent Tomorrow

Implementation plan approved in the work chat. Continue only from the recovered Chapter Six departure `chapter-seven:hollow-sun`. Nell has refused the Emperor's remembered morning, the Emperor and Alban are dead, Tomas is safe and Ansel cannot return. The older final-chapter notes promising an uncured Nell's survival or another perfect-morning offer are superseded.

## Journey and discoveries

- **The hollow sill:** the survey ship holds a crossing beneath the suspended ocean. Nell's fragment opens it; the Benefactor confronts the companions at the far stair, orders them back and withdraws through the suspended water before exploration begins. Alda holds the mortal end. All five companions travel; the actual one-to-three formation remains the player's choice.
- **The sea of taken mornings:** a familiar railway pier is embedded in an ocean's underside. Divine hounds repeat the original pursuit. Living captives remember the collection at Rillbridge. The collector is a divine predecessor of the empire's pressure guardian, not a resurrected officer. Its defeat releases a group of people; they walk toward the crossing.
- **The first harvest:** the festival sun and sheaves predate the empire. A founding-era farmer describes grain ripening through frost while the next valley went dark. The Benefactor moved days between places, then kept the people whose days it took. It accepted the dynasty's pledge and enlarged its reach deliberately. A furnace carrier preserves the harvest indefinitely. Breaking it frees its workers and extinguishes one stream feeding the god.
- **The quiet ward:** the original hospice is held at the instant after a healing. Patients want to leave; the Benefactor counts their later lives as part of its gift. Celia treats a patient using ordinary practice while the party defeats the mirrored engine. The healing was real, and the imprisonment was a choice. A second stream breaks.
- **The city of seasons:** Namar's terraces meet Skeld's snow and a river stair. The god has folded distant days together to preserve its possessions. Kit marks a route for the freed people, Petra protects the crossing and Orrin makes a return gate stay open without the divine supply. A winter dragon guards the remaining source from the near side of the closed return gate. A separate optional divine patrol protects a courier fitting.
- **The unwritten hour:** freed people organize water, bedding and return journeys. Supplies/rest, final equipment and short companion conversations remain available; earlier domain areas can be revisited. Kit and Nell get a practical, innocent moment, with Nell openly welcoming Kit’s company. No repeated Emperor offer.
- **The sun throne:** the Benefactor claims the captives as the lives it made possible. The party has severed the supply, so it cannot rebuild its body forever. Three saved victories: the monumental figure and its hands, the disembodied hollow sun, then its exposed heart after Nell returns the fragment. All use the normal combat/targeting rules and readable warnings. Each transformation commits on scene completion/skip; interrupted scenes replay and rewards never repeat. Equipment/preparation remains possible between forms through the last refuge, without resetting progress.
- **Home:** surviving captives and stolen futures return; ordinary deaths and previous choices remain. The divine claim is destroyed permanently. Larkspur's station reopens without a blessing. Tomas, Nell and their friends take the ordinary steam train to the real sea. Playable homecoming and seaside epilogue end the campaign, with a completion save.

## Combat and presentation

This is a substantial boss sequence, not a speed gauntlet. Familiar bosses keep their actual tactical identities and gain authored late-game numbers, domain scenery, dialogue and musical context. Human antagonists never resurrect. Each source defeat immediately starts its aftermath and removes its marker. Rest, supplies, optional manually fitted loot and retreat between domain areas prevent attrition from replacing tactics.

Native polygonal SVG scenery, real Tiled collision/arrivals/casts and the existing shared chunky raster. Promote the accepted animated Benefactor art into shared campaign/workshop assets. Give its sun and heart their own forms. All images remain below 128 KiB. Music stays procedural and 31-EDO, developing the approved radiant octatonic and its inversion, the descending divine call and selected familiar musical phrases. Distinct exploration, reprise battle, final-form and return cues; expressive held lines, quiet-bar sustains and smooth releases.
