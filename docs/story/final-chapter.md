# Planned final chapter — The God Who Lent Tomorrow

This is the end of the seven-chapter outline, not currently playable content. It follows the emperor's full defeat and a deliberate final departure. The user's requested ending is a journey into the god's own domain and its permanent defeat, restoring peace once and for all.

## Why there is still a final journey

The palace engine contains a conduit into the Benefactor's domain. The emperor's merger gives him control over the mortal side but never destroys or absorbs the whole god. The Benefactor has already destroyed Alban’s flagship, sent hunters and made Nell ill in Chapter Five. Breaking the vessel lets its collection spread beyond the empire; this escalates the known threat. The party has already learned this distinction in Chapter Four and prepared the path while severing anchors in Chapter Five.

The title is literal: the empire borrowed miraculous prosperity from future days, and the god demands repayment in human lives and futures. The god's claim persists beyond the dynasty. This is a power to break at its source, not an accounting puzzle solved with another document or a technicality.

## Final departure and route

1. **The returned capital:** survivors reunite with Tomas and the supporting cast. Confirm which towns and people were helped. Let the player finish optional business, rest and save before choosing to go.
2. **The hollow sun:** Nell's fragment opens the crossing. The airship crew and mortal allies hold the route open while the available companions enter. If Nell’s optional recovery was skipped, she remains safe with the medical crew while the others carry her fragment. Develop the damaged sun motif from Chapter One into the domain's full image. The god first spoke during Chapter Five’s intervention; its body is first revealed here.
3. **The sea of taken mornings:** walk beneath a suspended ocean. Free people whose days became part of the domain. Establish that the stolen future can be recovered without reversing deaths caused by ordinary violence.
4. **The first harvest:** see the founding bargain as a playable place. The Benefactor made the first offer, knew its cost and deliberately widened mortal dependence. The emperor continued the bargain and made his own choices; neither antagonist excuses the other.
5. **The city of seasons:** stolen days from different places meet. Resolve the companions' final contributions through distinct actions: Orrin breaks a transfer mechanism, Petra protects freed people while the route fails, and Celia restores people's ability to act without renewing the god's protection.
6. **The offered morning:** the Benefactor offers Nell Larkspur before the arrest, with Ansel alive and Tomas beside her. She recognizes what that world excludes: the people she met and the choices they have made. Her refusal is grounded in those relationships.
7. **The source of collection:** the party severs the bonds that join mortal days to the god's body. The anchor adventures taught them how. Nell or her companions return the fragment, opening the heart and surrendering its supernatural advantage.
8. **The final battle:** confront the Benefactor itself. A recognizable divine figure becomes the hollow sun, then exposes the vulnerable heart the party has worked to reach. Terrain and encounter forms should embody the known rules of the domain; decide their tactical details when implementation reaches this chapter. There is no new antagonist behind it.
9. **Return the days:** stolen futures and surviving captives are released. The god's capacity to collect is destroyed, and the path back opens. No remnant, replacement emperor or hidden contract restarts the threat.
10. **An ordinary departure:** the world rebuilds without effortless imperial miracles. Ansel remains dead. Tomas is home. Nell takes an ordinary steam train with her brother and companions for a holiday by the sea.

## What the ending must deliver

The god's defeat restores lasting peace. Allow a hopeful ending without pretending every building, death and hardship has been undone. Freed communities keep their relationships and remember the rescue. Steam engineering, ordinary healing and cooperation give the world practical alternatives. Nell survives whether or not the optional recovery quest was completed. If still ill, the end of the god’s harmful pulse restores her; she can live an ordinary life; her borrowed power ends with the bargain.
