# Chapter Six — The Emperor’s Last Day: capital finale

Approved scope: finish the capital assault and Emperor climax. Chapter Seven is the separate journey into the Benefactor’s domain. Ill Nell stays aboard and never answers through a signal link. The playable remembered Larkspur morning is a reward for completing Iria’s treatment, including if Nell is in reserve. Skip the Emperor’s offer completely on the ill route.

## Route

1. Allied quay → Stillwater Avenue: Skeld’s pilots, Namar’s launches and the earlier medical/railway crews support evacuation. A woman and her handcart repeat the same passage. Defeat the procession machines, physically free the street’s return and see the residents reach the boats. Marble, gardens and imperial ceremony conceal unfinished ordinary lives.
2. Palace of the Last Day: living servants repeat work while the Emperor’s remembered household cannot answer new questions. The remaining palace commandant obstructs evacuation; divine hunters attack the imperial defenses and the escape route. Petra confronts the commandant. A side encounter offers manually fitted loot; a shelter and supplies stay usable.
3. Recovered Nell alone receives the Larkspur morning: walk the station square, ask remembered Ansel an unanswered question, choose to leave. No resurrection. Ill Nell gets neither the scene nor remote dialogue. Her companions continue the assault without speaking for her.
4. Final preparations: Celia has examined Nell aboard and sees her pulse following the palace conduit. An uncured route explicitly offers a return to Iria before committing. Save a separate `capital-return` checkpoint before the assault, without overwriting manual slots. There is no countdown. Crossing the final threshold commits the route; Nell’s illness is never mistaken for consent.
5. Crown platform: an upward-facing, multi-contact Emperor vessel and its two destructible supports. Removing supports exposes the core; a readable round-fixed pattern briefly restores fallen supports before a later opening. All legal formations can win, with ordinary Taunt/Dazzle/guard and one-hit area attacks. The Emperor is decisively defeated in either outcome.
6. Recovered route: the broken conduit fails to bind Nell. The capital lands; living residents escape. The fragment opens a route toward the hollow sun. Revisit allies, rest and deliberately choose the Chapter Seven departure. Chapter Seven itself is not implemented by this change.
7. Uncured route: the Benefactor reaches Nell through her existing binding; her berth empties. Above the ruined crown, the Lightbearer forms around recognizable Nell and her lantern. It is a possessed mortal manifestation, never the god’s final body. A second distinct boss corrupts lantern attacks; Nell’s visible resistance interrupts the assault and gives companions openings. Kit acts on those openings; affection does not cure her.
8. Lightbearer victory destroys the local manifestation and consumes the fragment. Nell is lost. A short playable Larkspur aftermath shows Tomas and the surviving companions with her empty seat. The capital survives, collection continues elsewhere, and no domain departure is granted. The ending offers the preserved pre-assault checkpoint.

## Progress and persistence

Capital approach and exploration remain revisitable until final commitment. A mid-capital Iria cure preserves every earned flag, formation, resource and loadout, and enables the morning scene before commitment. Reserve Nell appears in recovered scenes but is never forced into the formation. No automatic healing, cure, loadout or party-size reset occurs on entry. Rewards are once-only bag items.

Cutscenes commit at completion/skip, interrupted scenes replay, and battles award once. Boss defeat alone does not grant chapter completion; the landing/aftermath does. Both seals and local progression are validated on import. A good departure is a current-format Chapter Six save at `chapter-seven:hollow-sun`; the bad ending remains a terminal Chapter Six checkpoint with no boundary. Ordinary battle defeat offers retry rather than advancing either ending.
