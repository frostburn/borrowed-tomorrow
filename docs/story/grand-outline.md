# Borrowed Tomorrow — the whole adventure

This is the agreed direction for the story reboot. Chapters One–Five are playable with bundled vector artwork, including all three freely ordered anchors and Alban’s flagship finale. Chapter Six’s sealed-capital opening and Namar desert expedition are also playable. Its northern/jungle routes, Emperor climax and Chapter Seven remain a plan to develop with the user. Future implementation can change routes and supporting characters while preserving the central pursuit, the emperor's defeat, and the separate final journey into the god's domain.

## The promise

Seventeen-year-old Nell wants to leave her provincial railway town and see the sea with her older brother, Tomas. Imperial soldiers take him during Larkspur's festival. A fleck of the crystal he stole catches in Nell's lantern. It hums, and the handle feels strangely cold. With Orrin, a young mechanic, she pursues the prison trains.

The pursuit becomes a rebellion, a rescue that succeeds while the world changes, a choice to fight back, an assault on an emperor becoming a god, and finally a journey beyond the mortal world to defeat the god itself. Railways connect a broad fantasy continent: forests, mines, rival kingdoms, ancient temples, a coast and an airship journey. The tone has warmth, humor, frightening discoveries and room to enjoy traveling with the party.

## What the title means

The empire's patron is worshipped as **the Benefactor**. It lent the empire power: abundant harvests, healing and crystal engines. The founding dynasty pledged future generations' days in exchange. Every miracle brought the empire's prosperity forward and enlarged what the god would eventually take back. The present emperor continued drawing on the bargain, telling himself he could find a way out before collection began.

The stopped towns are the first repayments. Their people did not consent to the bargain. Breaking a local collector releases its captives, but does not recover the years already taken or end the god's claim. Stopping the extraction is a real local victory; ending the source of it requires the final chapter.

The god's body beneath the palace is a conduit into its domain, rather than its whole existence. The empire calls the restraints a prison and thinks it has mastered its patron. The Benefactor permitted the arrangement because the conduit makes the empire dependent and gives it access to mortal lives. Begin with imperial worship, an unexplained lantern hum and a damaged sun mural, without divine speech or a boss cutaway; establish the distinction between conduit and source well before the finale.

## The emperor

The emperor lost his family in a civil war. He wants to preserve the last peaceful day before their deaths. Having failed to repay or escape the bargain, he intends to absorb the palace conduit and stop time permanently. He believes no future will mean no further deaths and nothing left for the Benefactor to collect. His paradise replaces living people's choices with the lives he remembers or wishes they had.

He is an active antagonist throughout the adventure. Show his orders, his interventions and the visible cost of his decisions. He offers Nell the morning before the raid, with Ansel alive and Tomas home. Her initial wish to restore her old life makes that offer a genuine temptation.

## Chapter One — Leaving Home

**Goal:** catch the soldiers who took Tomas. **Local antagonist:** Captain Alban.

Let the player meet Tomas at the festival after a short opening scene. He has returned from the capital and is hiding something. Imperial crystal lamps, the festival blessing and the siblings' promised trip to the sea establish the world through ordinary life. Orrin's independent steam engine is an eccentric work in progress.

Alban arrives to arrest Tomas for stealing a crystal from the palace engine. Stationmaster Ansel, Nell's mentor, intervenes and is fatally wounded. The crystal fractures as Tomas tries to keep it from the captain. A pale fleck catches in Nell's lantern glass. A low resonance travels through its handle and gives her the shivers. Neither a voice nor a demonstrated time stop explains it. Tomas is taken during the struggle. Ansel's final action gives her a way out of the square.

Nell fights through the pursuit hounds, finds Orrin in the western workshop and chooses to pursue Tomas. No component-collection errands stand between the decision and the journey. Orrin joins, bringing a steam lance and an engine that can use the old branch.

At Rillbridge, a living village has been stopped by an imperial collector. The party confronts its guard, breaks the collector and wakes the villagers. Captain Alban's signal calls the operation a repayment. The rescued villagers have lost days and some of their strength; that damage is not silently reversed. The sun emblem on the collector matches the festival lamps. The lantern hums more strongly nearby, but the party does not claim to understand its powers or why they can move in the village.

At the mountain pass, Alban blocks pursuit. He has already sent Tomas east in an armored prison coach. Nell and Orrin defeat him, open the route and escape on their own engine. Alban survives and retreats; the chapter's obstacle has genuinely been overcome. The destination is specific: the prison-train exchange at the Iron Coast. End on Nell and Orrin deciding to follow. Keep the god offstage and silent.

**Progression:** short festival exploration → staged raid → solo escape battle → recruit Orrin → frozen village and collector battle → mountain-pass boss → saved departure. One optional workshop fight rewards equipment. Rest and basic supplies are available without errands. Rescued people acknowledge the result on return visits.

## Chapter Two — Prison Trains

**Goal:** find Tomas before the next transfer. **Payoff:** free the prisoners and learn his destination.

The pursuit reaches the Iron Coast exchange and Cinderbank mining settlement. Kit, a registered courier Nell’s own age, is delivering medicine and letters when Alban seizes his bag and tears his permit. Nell sees the interference before meeting him. He guides her toward Tomas’s coach to recover his deliveries; helping the prisoners and their families draws him into the larger pursuit. He sometimes gets flustered when Nell thanks him; the early hints remain brief here, before the later optional-cure affection. Kit is the only new traveling companion here. Celia’s introduction and recruitment remain at Breakwater harbor in Chapter Four. Introduce Petra, an imperial knight pursuing the fragment, initially a rival with a sincere belief in the empire’s protection.

The raid succeeds: prisoners escape and Nell receives Tomas’s transfer slip naming the palace engine works. Dena signs for Kit’s recovered medicine and takes the prisoners in after the hospital refused them. Orrin can take the medical coaches straight to the capital. Nell chooses an extra run down the branch to collect Cinderbank’s families; Kit’s remaining letter belongs to a child whose father is already aboard.

Alban orders Cinderbank collected for the capital. Petra objects, then follows his authority while requesting protection for the patients. Orrin opens the collector. The player holds the loading walk while Dena brings the families aboard. Blocked families wait safely; three clear rounds or defeating all the machines completes the rescue. Kit delivers the letter as the girl reunites with her father.

Petra allows the patients passage but insists on arresting Nell and recovering the fragment. At the final crossing Alban retreats behind her shield. Petra is the chapter boss: Taunt draws attacks to her, Shield bash pushes without consuming her main action, and Saber sweep punishes clustering. Winning disarms her and opens the crossing.

Alban then orders fire on the departing coaches. Petra steps into the firing line and refuses, losing her commission. She stays to countermand the order among her soldiers while the medical train leaves. Orrin’s coal engine carries everyone without imperial crystals; Petra sees it work. This first refusal costs her position, but her public break and deliberate recruitment remain in Chapter Three.

## Chapter Three — Beneath the Palace

**Goal:** rescue Tomas and disable extraction. **Payoff:** Tomas comes home alive. **Reversal:** the emperor merges with the conduit.

The medical clearance admits the train to the capital’s south siding. Alban offers Petra her commission back if she seals the coaches; Petra recalls his firing order, breaks his command rod, refuses to surrender them and protects the coach doors while Nell’s party defeats the custody machines. She joins permanently afterward. Celia remains deferred. Four travelers may now choose three active fighters in Equipment; no story explanation for that limit is established here.

Tomas has been holding a valve open from captivity, disabling the outer alarm and creating the party’s opening. Interrupt the engine warden’s execution order, defeat it and free his restraints. Give the siblings a substantial reunion, including Tomas learning of Ansel’s death. The ward staff take Tomas to the coal engine; he remains safe and never becomes a combat unit.

Tomas explains that the collectors send people’s days into the restrained vessel below the palace. His bypass gives the party a choice: cut the crystal feeds and release a regulator shield by occupying both earth plates, or vent the pressure line and fight crows among steam outlets. An optional stores patrol and five manually equipped rewards support different companion builds.

The emperor appears in person and refuses to stop collection, citing the capital’s dependence. The party defeats the heart regulator and shuts the feeds. In response, he chooses to enter the vessel rather than let the engines fail. The capital rises and time begins failing outside it. Nell escapes aboard the medical train with Tomas, the passengers and her companions. The rescue remains a permanent accomplishment; defeating the emperor and later the god remain distinct future goals.

## Chapter Four — A Light on the Water

**Goal:** reopen the harbor and release the coast from the capital's signal. **Payoff:** the rescue becomes a voluntary counterattack, with a first plan for reaching the Emperor.

The medical train returns to the Iron Coast refuge. Tomas and Dena are settling the same rescued passengers; the four travelers have stayed together. Bram's ferry crews are stranded safely behind the sea gate. Nell takes blankets to the pump house, where Celia is treating them. Celia joins to stop the signal causing their injuries, without changing the player's formation.

Choose a dry chain walk or shallow pump floor to defeat the gate machines, then wind the gate open by hand. The crews come home and Bram starts supply runs. Climb the service stair to Celia's old sanctuary hospice. Its bronze astronomical ring is wired to the capital; the former hospice warden now obeys the Emperor's preservation order. Clear the return-line bridge, disconnect the imperial cable and defeat its local reserve. The wave falls and the coast moves again.

Behind an imperial plate, Celia discovers the missing terms of the hospice oath: the dynasty pledged descendants' days for the Benefactor's boons. The healing was real, but the people being charged never agreed to the price. An operating chart identifies the three distant anchors—Hammerplain, Dawnwatch and Windmere—supporting the palace conduit. An older diagram places a source beyond the hollow sun. The party copies the evidence; the god stays silent.

Nell and Tomas take their promised walk to the sea. Her original pursuit has succeeded. She chooses to go after the anchors, while he keeps the rescued communities in contact. Celia leaves a complete copy of the pledge with Dena. Bram knows a survey airship at the western airfield. The three active fighters remain a mechanical choice throughout, without a story explanation. End at the saved `chapter-five:anchors` departure.

## Chapter Five — Taking the World Back

**Goal:** sever the anchors and assemble an assault. **Payoff:** liberated regions and an alliance the player helped create.

Acquire the survey airship at the western airfield in a shared opening. Four acts follow: **Hammerplain, Dawnwatch and Windmere in any order**, then **Alban's flagship** after all three. Use a compact landing chart and a traveling home aboard the ship. Each region stops further collection and supplies something the capital assault needs.

At Hammerplain, Orrin and the foundry workers save independent engines from a mobile furnace carrier. At Dawnwatch, Petra protects the lower town, defeats her former commander and earns the help of soldiers who refuse to fire on their neighbors. At Windmere, Celia and the hospital staff replace the ward's dependence on an anchor before defeating its mirrored ward engine. The healing was real; hiding its price was the betrayal. The three acts have distinct routes, bosses, rewards and changed return visits. None restores days already taken.

Earlier rescues supply crews, medicine and intelligence. Kit's courier contacts connect the alliance. After two regional victories, the Emperor offers Nell a preserved Larkspur morning with Ansel alive; she is tempted without anyone being resurrected. Alban's cordon becomes the fourth-act obstacle. Fight upward from the survey ship against his enormous flagship: destroy its two batteries separately, survive the boarding troops, then expose the bridge. On the command deck Petra confronts Alban, but the Benefactor intervenes first. A bodiless voice and seam of light accuse the party of breaking its anchors. The god destroys Alban, his men and the hull. Kit pulls Nell across the gangway as the shard’s pulse makes her collapse; Celia catches her. The Benefactor’s final form remains unseen.

Divine hunters retaliate at the survey ship’s medical hatch. Nell is too ill to fight; the remaining companions drive them off. Kit takes Celia’s notes for Sera and stays beside Nell while Alda holds for the capital’s outer mooring. The Emperor's defeat remains Chapter Six; the god's own domain remains Chapter Seven. See [chapter-five-plan.md](chapter-five-plan.md) for the four-act design and the boundary between the dev preview and future campaign work.

## Chapter Six — The Emperor's Last Day

**Goal:** stop the emperor and release the mortal world. **Payoff:** the human antagonist's complete defeat.

Nell remains aboard under medical care until the optional Iria treatment. Her companions lead while she is unavailable; if recovered first, she participates in the foreign journeys and capital assault. Kit’s delivery of Celia’s notes brings Sera’s lead to a jungle river settlement experienced in crystal-exposure treatment. The playable Iria expedition brings healer Ines and launch pilot Tavi together with Celia to restore Nell. It can precede, interrupt or follow Namar’s desert journey. Restore Nell without destroying the fragment or forcing her into the active formation. The capital remains playable if treatment is deferred; knowingly crossing its final threshold untreated leads to the Lightbearer bad ending; its personal scenes make Nell’s warmth clearer, with a brief hug after treatment and hand-holding at the real sea. See [chapter-six-plan.md](chapter-six-plan.md).

The first approach fails when the Emperor seals the capital. Two seals belong to a defensive pact with allied kingdoms in the desert and icy north. Open both destinations in either order, alongside the optional jungle recovery route. In the desert, a divided court leads to a palace escape, occupied observatory and sun-serpent guardian before the ruler publicly breaks the seal. In the north, join a local expedition through glacier wrecks to a sanctuary occupied by a winter dragon; the kingdom contributes ships and navigators. Both seals must fall before the landing bridge reaches the imperial city. The Emperor has used the intervening time to complete his preserved capital.

Allied forces support the approach and evacuation. Familiar places have become fragments of the emperor's remembered paradise. His dead loved ones repeat conversations while the living inhabitants lose their ability to choose or act. Allow the player to recognize individual people in that process.

The emperor becomes a monstrous divine vessel. The party uses the knowledge and allies earned across the journey to sever its supports and expose its heart. Defeat him in a full climax with his human motive still recognizable. On the recovered route the fragment is retained for its final use in Chapter Seven. Only recovered Nell is drawn into the remembered Larkspur morning. She and Tomas take the sea train together; remembering the people still losing their days, she forces herself awake and refuses the offer. The defeated Emperor is dead and has no final speech. Ill Nell stays aboard; there is no remote signal or answer, and the offer is skipped.

The conduit breaks. The capital returns to earth, and the emperor's grip ends. The hollow sun opens above the world. The Benefactor expands its already-declared retaliation through the broken conduit and demands repayment for every harvest, healing and engine it supplied. Days start being taken from regions the empire never ruled. The emperor's attempt to evade the bargain has brought collection forward, rather than eliminating the lender.

With Nell recovered, the broken current fails to bind her and the intact fragment reveals a route through the hollow sun. Reunite with supporting crews, finish personal business and deliberately choose the final departure. If the player explicitly proceeded uncured despite Celia’s warning and the still-open Iria route, the Benefactor takes Nell as the Lightbearer. Its boss corrupts her lantern attacks; her hand sometimes closes the shutter and exposes the mantle. Victory ends the local manifestation but Nell is lost and the fragment crumbles. A playable Larkspur aftermath ends at her empty seat on the sea train. Collection continues. This bad ending has no domain departure and offers the preserved pre-assault save.

## Chapter Seven — The God Who Lent Tomorrow

**Goal:** enter the Benefactor's domain, recover the stolen days and end its power to collect. **Payoff:** lasting peace, with a future belonging to the people who live it.

On the recovered route, Nell’s intact fragment opens the path through the hollow sun. The uncured Lightbearer ending does not reach this chapter. The party travels beyond the mortal world, through landscapes built from days the god has taken: an ocean hanging over a night sky, the ruins of the empire's first miraculous harvest, and streets where different stolen seasons meet. These places reveal the history of the bargain through people and events. They are not a replay of every old dungeon.

Meet people from earlier collected settlements. The party helps them act and move again, making the promised recovery visible before the final battle. The Benefactor treats the rescued people as its possessions and their release as theft. Nell has already refused the remembered morning in the capital; there is no repeated offer. Familiar pressure, furnace, mirror and winter guardians defend the stolen places and reveal how imperial machinery grew around the god’s gifts.

The original anchors taught the party how the god joins mortal days to its body. In the domain, the companions sever those bonds at their source. Nell or her companions return the fragment to expose the divine heart; its borrowed power ends. Fight the Benefactor itself in multiple forms; the final transformation draws on the damaged sun motif introduced in Chapter One and developed in later chapters. Victory destroys its capacity to claim people's lives. It cannot return through another emperor or renew the bargain.

Release the stolen days and surviving captives. Do not use a reset to erase the adventure: Ansel and people killed by violence remain dead, decisions and relationships remain, and towns still need rebuilding. Divine engines and effortless miracles cease. Ordinary skill, cooperation and the alternatives developed by Orrin's region make a livable future possible.

End at Larkspur station. Tomas is home. Nell boards an ordinary steam train with her brother and friends for a holiday by the sea. The first morning after their return has no imperial blessing and needs none.

The desert uses neutral-interval 31-EDO, the north Orwell[9] with optional Myna[7] for glacier ruins, and the jungle Slendric[5]/[6]. The domain uses an overtone-derived octatonic and its inversion, also in 31-EDO. See [music-direction.md](../music-direction.md) for exact degrees, notation and composition intent.

## Why a young protagonist can win

The fragment gives Nell access to the conflict and a limited shelter from stopped time. It does not make her stronger than the god. Experience and equipment develop her combat ability; companions supply skills she lacks; the guardians explain vulnerabilities; freed communities make the assault possible. Their combined actions create the opening for the final strike. Introduce the final solution's ingredients throughout the journey.

## Rules for later chapters

- The immediate objective names a person, place or action the party cares about. Completing it changes the situation and earns the next destination.
- Give each chapter a local victory, a recognizable antagonist and a consequential discovery. A mysterious letter is not sufficient payment for a chapter of errands.
- Stage the major decisions and reversals with actors, camera movement, sound and playable action. Keep dialogue concise and let players advance or skip it.
- Keep active combat parties to three members. A larger traveling cast can participate in scenes, while reserves retain their own levels and equipment.
- Keep the party's ordinary affection, arguments and humor present between crises. Allow exploration and rest; narrative urgency is not a hidden real-time timer.
- Foreshadow through worship, old imagery and unsettling sensory clues; let the god speak only after later discoveries earn that encounter. The emperor and the Benefactor have related goals but distinct agency. Defeating each resolves a different part of the threat.
- Protect the completed rescue of Tomas and every local victory. The finale restores stolen futures without erasing the cost of the journey.

Nell and Kit’s revised affection follows [their arc notes](nell-kit-arc.md), including the recovery gate and real-sea payoff.

Petra’s accountability continues through [her arc notes](petra-arc.md): a Cinderbank passenger declines her help, she sends Dena her own account after Alban’s death, and she commits to answer the families in person after the sea trip. Neither her service nor the final victory grants a pardon.
