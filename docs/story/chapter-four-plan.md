# Chapter Four — A Light on the Water

The rewritten chapter continues the version-10 `chapter-four:broken-world` departure. Tomas, the medical passengers and Nell's four-person traveling roster escaped together. There is no injury reset, scattered-party search or forced local formation. The three-fighter cap is only a game rule.

## Playable progression

1. Return to the railway refuge. Tomas and Dena are settling the same passengers. Bram's ferry crews are safe aboard their boat, but imperial gate machines prevent them entering the harbor. Nell volunteers to take Dena's blankets to the pump house.
2. Meet Celia while she splints a dockhand's wrist. The gate horn repeats the emperor's order to hold all coastal departures by the Emperor’s order. Celia worked in the sanctuary hospice above the harbor; she joins to stop the cause of the injuries. Recruitment preserves the player's active formation. Equipment can change it immediately.
3. Choose the dry chain walk (guardian and ranged crows) or shallow pump floor (guardian and hounds, slowing water). Defeat the gate guards, then turn the winding wheel. The gate clears the route; the ferry stays at its Breakwater berth while Bram brings the crews to Dena and prepares supply runs. The cart patrol can be declined.
4. Climb the service stair to the sanctuary. Its old astronomical ring is wired to the capital. A warden that once opened the hospice now maintains the coastal arrest. Clear the eastern bridge guards, physically disconnect the return cable, then defeat the warden's local reserve.
5. The coast moves again. Beneath an imperial cover plate, Celia finds the missing terms of the hospice oath: the founders pledged their descendants' days. An operating chart identifies Hammerplain, Dawnwatch and Windmere as anchor stations supporting the conduit. An older diagram distinguishes that conduit from a source beyond the hollow sun. Copy both; the god never speaks.
6. Return to the supplied refuge. Nell and Tomas take their promised walk to the sea. Her rescue has succeeded; staying is a real option. Nell chooses to pursue the anchors, while Tomas keeps the rescued communities in contact. Bram knows a survey ship at the western airfield. Prepare freely or commit `chapter-five:anchors`.
