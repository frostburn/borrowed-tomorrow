# Chapter Four — The Broken World

Continue the version-10 `chapter-four:broken-world` departure. Tomas and the medical passengers escaped the capital; none is taken again. A new pulse stopped the western line on their return to the Iron Coast ward. Nell wakes there with Tomas. Orrin and Kit are evacuating the nearby Breakwater works; Petra has gone up the sanctuary road with stranded patients.

## Playable progression

1. Nell wakes beside Tomas at the railway ward. Their pursuit succeeded. Tomas keeps the passengers safe and organizes messages while Nell looks for their friends.
2. Play Orrin with Kit at the Breakwater works. Choose the dry service walk or the flooded pump floor, defeat the collection machines, release the evacuation gate and bring the workers out. An additional stores patrol can be declined. Existing equipment remains fitted.
3. Play Petra on the sanctuary road. Celia is treating people whom the imperial machines are trying to return to the stopped valley. Petra protects them; Celia joins her after a staged introduction. After their fight, play Celia's short search of the old sanctuary seal, then escort the patients to Nell and Tomas.
4. Reunite all five recruited travelers. Restore the player's previous three-person formation; Celia begins in reserve. Equipment can then change the formation normally. No story explanation for the maximum size is invented.
5. Nell's party enters the sanctuary, defeats its warden and releases the local arrest. The old guardians' record shows three distant anchors holding the palace conduit steady. Those anchors still need to be broken in Chapter Five. The hollow-sun image establishes that the Benefactor's source lies beyond the vessel and can survive it. The god remains silent.
6. At the ward, Nell deliberately chooses to lead the counterattack. Tomas stays with the resistance and coordinates the rescued communities. End at `chapter-five:anchors`, with the western airfield as the next destination. Ansel remains dead.
