# Chapter Six — Namar and the western pact seal

Implementation scope: the shared sealed-capital opening and a complete desert expedition. The northern seal, jungle treatment and Emperor climax remain upcoming routes within Chapter Six. Finishing Namar does not complete the chapter or cure Nell.

## Route

1. **The outer mooring.** The survey crew extends a landing bridge. The Emperor closes a barrier in front of it, with living people still visible inside. He has invoked an old mutual-defense pact held at two foreign capitals. Alda keeps a safe berth outside the capital. Sera's reply to Celia identifies river healers who treat crystal exposure; the jungle lead is recorded without pretending that the journey is implemented.
2. **Namar at dusk.** Step into a working city of canals, awnings, rooftop gardens and postal boats. Amal runs the evening exchange and offers to forward Kit's letters, including an ordinary report to Tomas. Orrin notices that the city's water clock works without a crystal. The queen has called an audience; no preliminary delivery errand gates it.
3. **The divided court.** Samira recognizes her own grain orders in the ledger, hears what happened to Alban and asks why the Emperor has not admitted her ward crews. She decides to withdraw the seal and prepare her own boats and stores. Sahir fears that withdrawing protection would doom Namar. He orders the observatory guardian awakened while his captain turns the palace cordon on the queen. Samira accompanies the escape as a supporting actor.
4. **Out through the palace.** Choose the gallery cordon or the shallow service channel. The guards are shown before commitment, both routes lead to the same escape, and supplies remain available. Amal opens the postal stair from outside. No hostage timer or new abduction. The minister's action changes the city and palace art; the market residents shelter safely.
5. **Above the roofs.** Ascend the observatory's terraces. Divine glasswings attack the machinery independently of Sahir's orders. Clear the ascent, then reach a sheltered rest bench and an optional instrument chest before the boss. The queen can operate the original bronze release once the guardian is stopped.
6. **The sun serpent.** Face upward toward a colossal living serpent coiled around a bronze solar apparatus. Its anchored head covers three hexes; two targetable coils alternately expose themselves. Breaking coils weakens the head's armor and stops the corresponding reflected attack lanes. Its breath leaves glass that contributes clearly announced reflections to its next attack. Taunt redirects the attack; Dazzle, guard, whole-body targeting and one-hit-per-unit areas retain their normal rules. The core is always damageable, so players can choose the risk of concentrating on it.
7. **A public break.** Samira takes the lifted band and ledger to the evening exchange, owns her signed grain orders and opens the palace granaries for relief before breaking the western seal. Sahir survives under city custody; his fear does not erase the attempted seizure. The kingdom supplies navigators, water and evacuation barges. The party returns to a usable city and safe ship, keeping all progress. The second seal remains visibly intact. Nell is still ill, awake and able to receive the news. Later conversations only report her letter dispatched if the player actually posted it.
