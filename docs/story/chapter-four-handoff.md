## What happened

The medical clearance worked. Petra broke with Alban rather than surrender the passengers and joined after the custody battle. Tomas held a valve open to disable the outer alarm, giving the party its opening. The party stopped his execution and freed his restraints. Tomas had seen Ansel wounded; Nell confirmed that he did not survive after Tomas was taken away. Tomas explained the collectors’ connection to the vessel below the conduit works and bypassed the execution circuit before going to the medical train.

The party defeated the regulator and stopped extraction. The Emperor used the conduit’s remaining charge to teleport to the royal palace and ordered a coastal arrest through the sanctuary relay. He remained human and was not defeated in combat. The works stayed grounded; their machinery failed as the charge drained. Tomas, the coast prisoners and the Cinderbank families all escaped on Orrin’s coal engine. Petra stayed with the party. Alban survives; his later resolution remains planned for Chapter Five.
