# Chapter Six — Iria and Nell's recovery

Scope: the optional jungle river journey, playable before, during or after Namar. The northern expedition and Emperor climax remain later work. This route opens at the shared flight chart after Sera's referral. It breaks no pact seal, grants no Chapter Six ending and never starts a fever timer.

## Route and people

1. **Iria's river landing.** Ines, a senior local healer, has read Celia's notes. Her practice predates the imperial wards; treating crystal workers is ordinary work here. Tavi is a launch pilot preparing the community's treatment boat. Both have patients and obligations beyond Nell. Nell stays safely aboard while the party opens the way to the rain house.
2. **The canopy passage.** Imperial patrol machines are still blocking the old medical waterway. Clear the lower crossing, then open its boat chain by hand. Tavi can take the village patients and equipment upriver. The forest walkway has a rest shelter and an optional equipment cache; there is no rare-herb collection or timed escort.
3. **The rain house.** Divine hunters have occupied the old treatment terrace. Drive them off before bringing Nell across. Ines and Celia prepare the treatment together; Kit helps with the boat and the patient's belongings. The shard is kept intact while the harmful connection is interrupted. The cure is a specific completed treatment, not an ordinary rest or automatic battle reward.
4. **A seat by the river.** Nell wakes without the returning pain. Kit has arranged everything within reach and loses his words when she asks him to sit. Keep this brief, innocent and unresolved. Nell chooses to travel again. She becomes available in Equipment without displacing an active companion or changing her equipment. Iria, its clinic and the ship remain revisitable.

The route uses three directly authored SVG/Tiled exploration maps, two main encounters and one optional equipment encounter. Loot goes to the shared bag for manual fitting. Recovery preserves the actual formation, inventory, supplies, time and all desert progress. Only Nell's HP/focus are restored by the cure. `nellIll` remains historical; `nellRecovered` is permanent. No later rest, save/reload, boss scene or desert visit may make her ill again.
