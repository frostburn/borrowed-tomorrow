## Start with the pursuit

Arrive at the Iron Coast prison-train exchange. The player should see a train, a person or a recognizable sign of Tomas's passage promptly. Give Nell a concrete route toward the resistance's train raid. A helpful contact can introduce a conflict, but do not withhold basic information behind unrelated repairs or errands.

Introduce Kit, a registered courier and Nell’s peer, while he attempts an honest delivery. Alban seizes his medicine and letters and tears his permit on screen. Kit initially helps Nell recover those deliveries, then becomes involved in freeing the prisoners. He occasionally stumbles over his words around Nell, then gets back to helping the prisoners. Keep this an innocent, unresolved gag, with no romance payoff. Kit is the only new recruit. Save Celia’s introduction and recruitment for later. Introduce Petra, the imperial knight, through this pursuit. Petra remains loyal enough to defend Alban as Chapter Two’s boss. After defeat she refuses his order to fire on the coaches, losing her commission; her public break and permanent recruitment follow in Chapter Three. Introduce Dena at the ward, connect Kit’s deliveries to the rescued families and give the collector battle a loading-route objective. The chapter’s travelers are Nell, Orrin and Kit. The mechanical cap remains three and needs no story explanation. Their builds, equipment and portraits are previewable in the Vite workshop; that preview is not a Chapter One recruitment event. The first raid frees prisoners and earns Tomas's destination in the capital. It also gives the knight a visible reason to question the empire. The later rescue in Chapter Three must bring Tomas back alive and remain a permanent accomplishment.

## What carries forward

Ansel is dead, Iona keeps Larkspur open, Rillbridge’s remaining residents are awake and still feel the cost of the collection; Elspeth and Tilda are searching for those still missing. The three days are the collector’s recorded charge, not elapsed time in the train pursuit. Alban survived his defeat, but lost the crossing. Tomas is captive and has not joined the party. Nell's fragment is in Ansel's lantern; it is not an equipment purchase that may be sold or removed. The optional brass reflector improves that lantern without being the fragment itself.

Nell and Orrin know about local repayment but not the whole bargain. They have heard no divine voice and seen no palace cutaway. The lantern hums and its handle feels cold; neither character knows what the crystal can do or why they could move in Rillbridge. The temple, imperial conduit and original bargain require later discoveries. Foreshadow the Benefactor's own domain before the emperor's defeat; the separate final chapter must feel like the culmination of known danger.

Early supplies offer ordinary bandages and coastal equipment. Stronger field bandages and focus tonics are saved for Chapter Four medical suppliers. Iona, the coastal engine, Dena’s ward and Cinderbank’s medical coaches offer the shared spare-gear sale counter. Preserve purchased and remaining medical stacks in the inventory bag across this boundary; do not refill them. See [economy.md](../economy.md).
