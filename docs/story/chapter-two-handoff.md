## Start with the pursuit

Arrive at the Iron Coast prison-train exchange. The player should see a train, a person or a recognizable sign of Tomas's passage promptly. Give Nell a concrete route toward the resistance's train raid. A helpful contact can introduce a conflict, but do not withhold basic information behind unrelated repairs or errands.

Introduce Kit, a registered courier and Nell’s peer, while he attempts an honest delivery. Alban seizes his medicine and letters and tears his permit on screen. Kit initially helps Nell recover those deliveries, then becomes involved in freeing the prisoners. He occasionally stumbles over his words around Nell, then gets back to helping the prisoners. Keep this an innocent, unresolved gag, with no romance payoff. Kit is the only new recruit. Save Celia’s introduction and recruitment for later. Introduce Petra, the imperial knight, through this pursuit. Petra helps the escape in Chapter Two and permanently joins in Chapter Three after refusing to surrender the escapees. The chapter’s travelers are Nell, Orrin and Kit. The mechanical cap remains three; its story explanation is future work. Their builds, equipment and portraits are previewable in the Vite workshop; that preview is not a Chapter One recruitment event. The first raid frees prisoners and earns Tomas's destination in the capital. It also gives the knight a visible reason to question the empire. The later rescue in Chapter Three must bring Tomas back alive and remain a permanent accomplishment.

## What carries forward

Ansel is dead, Iona keeps Larkspur open, Rillbridge is awake, and its inhabitants still feel the cost of the collection. Alban survived his defeat, but lost the crossing. Tomas is captive and has not joined the party. Nell's fragment is in Ansel's lantern; it is not an equipment purchase that may be sold or removed. The optional brass reflector improves that lantern without being the fragment itself.

Nell and Orrin know about local repayment but not the whole bargain. They have heard no divine voice and seen no palace cutaway. The lantern hums and its handle feels cold; neither character knows what the crystal can do or why they could move in Rillbridge. The temple, imperial conduit and original bargain require later discoveries. Foreshadow the Benefactor's own domain before the emperor's defeat; the separate final chapter must feel like the culmination of known danger.
