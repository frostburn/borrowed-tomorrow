# Traveling together

The companions need relationships with each other as well as with Nell. Keep the differences between them: Kit remembers Petra barring the train; Celia and Orrin approach the lost miracles differently; Nell and Orrin knew Ansel before the others arrived. Friendship does not settle Petra's obligations to the Cinderbank families.
