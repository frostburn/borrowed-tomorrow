# Petra's armored rig

`src/art/petra-actor.ts` binds Petra's four native idle actors to the layered reference. Face, hair, armor, tabard, gloves, saber and shield retain their original RGB pixels. Each profile uses its own artwork; nothing is mirrored or downsampled. Idle actors and portraits stay unchanged.

`PETRA_PROPORTIONS` uses 9-pixel leg bones, an 18.8-pixel resting hip height, 0.55-pixel rise, 3.5-pixel hip spacing, a 24-pixel stride, 70% feet, 20-pixel shoulder height, 8.8-pixel shoulder spacing and 7.5/7-pixel arm bones. Leg width is 85%. The complete lower skeleton is registered at anatomical (-0.5, -2), including hip, knee, ankle and foot contact landmarks. The seat and thighs share a continuous projected green fabric field; leather greaves, shaped soles and complete dark leg contours preserve the native costume.

Each arm has separately registered native armor, cuff and glove cutouts. Rounded material backing fills foreshortened joints; the torso uses the original projected armor field rather than an enlarged repeating swatch. Native head and torso decals keep their original dimensions and follow the small hip rise. The saber attaches to the anatomical right grip, the shield to the left. Walking keeps the blade at a fixed carry angle: it follows the hand without independent rotation, avoiding diagonal-pixel wobble. An action can reveal the original round shield face or the complete far-side blade without mirroring Petra.

`petraAttackPose` has separate saber and shield-bash actions. Both brace the same two flat feet nine pixels apart along the facing axis and preserve rigid arm/leg lengths. Saber wind-up/contact drives the right arm while the left guards. Bash drives the left shield while the right carries the blade; Taunt uses the shield brace. The shared runtime supplies contact timing, recovery, reduced motion and callback cancellation; this does not change damage or turn rules.

Run `node scripts/export-petra-animation.mjs` with the existing Node/Vite and Python/Pillow dependencies. It prepares four 80 x 80 binary-alpha skins under `assets/characters/petra-skin/` and renders four small 160 x 400 pages under `public/art/animation/`. Frames 0–5 are the six-step walk, 6–7 saber wind-up/contact and 8–9 shield-bash wind-up/contact. Every frame uses the (40, 68) ground anchor. The exporter reads native idle art, never old animation pages or a large source sheet. Stage exports and run `npm run check:art-upload` before publishing.

Animation offers **Petra**, **Match Petra proportions** and a mannequin **Saber and shield (Petra)** preset. The **Right weapon** and **Left weapon** layers can be inspected/exported separately; pose JSON records both anatomical attachments and her independent measurements. Sprites offers **Walk**, **Attack** and **Bash**, with native playback, pause and frame stepping. Gameplay uses the baked pages for party actors, Petra's cutscene moves and her Chapter Two boss appearance. Enemy presentation receives the selected aim before knockback changes its victim's cell, walks the chosen route before the action, and resolves retaliation through the actor appearance. Authoring code and prepared skins remain dev-only; runtime keeps its own 24-pixel stride constant.
