## Runtime playback

The shared actor frame player animates exploration leaders/followers, staged cutscene moves and tactical paths. Nell translates her directional idle pose during all movement. Other walking follows distance: Orrin advances every 28/6 pixels (his rigged 28-pixel stride), Kit advances every 26/6 pixels, and Petra advances every 24/6 pixels. This preserves gait across adjacent cells and turns, scales to tactical/cutscene travel speeds, and stops cycling when an actor stops translating. Stopping restores the facing idle texture. Sprites previews all six poses at 80 ms each, with phase labels, pause and wraparound stepping; the Animation authoring review uses 120 ms. Attacks use 140 ms wind-up, contact through 280 ms, and recovery to 380 ms, retaining the previous battle input delay. Damage visuals, skill effects and player attack sounds begin at contact. Battle HP labels travel with their actors. Pending presentation callbacks check battle identity so forced outcomes and restarts cannot replay old hits. Retaliations are captured before round-end flag resets so counters still animate on the final turn of a round. Reduced-motion mode keeps idle artwork and shortens battle presentation.

The Vite Sprites workshop shares the same pages and frame sequence, with pause and frame stepping. Idle sprites, portraits, character rules and combat damage are unchanged. `tests/e2e/actor-motion.spec.ts` covers directions, native cropped pixels, tactical paths, all four heroes' attacks and Petra's shield bash and interruption. These detailed checks remain outside default CI.
