# Nell and Orrin motion frames

Nell's walk is baked from the accepted layered reference with her original native face, cap, braids and costume pixels attached as a skin. Orrin retains his generated directional walk art. Idle actors and portraits remain unchanged. Each direction has one 160 x 320 PNG page containing eight 80 x 80 frames. Frames 0–5 are a complete six-frame walk: left contact, left support, right passing, right contact, right support, left passing. Frames 6–7 retain the existing native wind-up/contact artwork. Ground anchor remains (40, 68). Neither side view is mirrored; equipment stays in the right hand through every pose.

`animation-sources.json` records Nell's rig/exporter, Orrin's selected six-pose sources and the original attack sources. Nell's deterministic authoring source is `src/art/nell-actor.ts`: twelve-pixel leg bones retarget her compact proportions while keeping the reference's foot targets, heel/toe roll, right-hand grip and 40-pixel stride. The textured native renderer samples source pixels without blending; transparent texels do not hide farther parts. Face, costume and braid pixels come from the four existing native idle sprites, with no new source atlas or downsampling of the face. See `nell-rig.md` for the layer/material recipe.

Run `node scripts/export-nell-animation.mjs` to rebuild Nell's four native pages from the rig and idle skins. It requires the existing development dependencies plus Python/Pillow and preserves the final native attack row verbatim. The Animation workshop's **Nell** skin uses the same geometry, textures and renderer, with independently visible/exportable body, limb and weapon layers. Gameplay consumes only the baked PNGs.

For Orrin, run `python3 scripts/character-walk-guides.py /path/to/references` to reproduce the generation guides, then `python3 scripts/import-character-animation.py /path/to/generated_images` to repeat his native export. It removes low-alpha backdrop haze, isolates the silhouette, uses one scale per direction and fixed cap registration, samples without blending or palette reduction, and removes detached fragments. It retains the final attack row and does not overwrite Nell's rigged walk. `walk-prompts.json` retains the original generation briefs. Source sheets stay outside git; each committed page is below 53 KB.

## Runtime playback

The shared actor frame player animates exploration leaders/followers, staged cutscene moves and tactical paths. Walking follows distance: Nell advances one pose per 40/6 native pixels (a 40-pixel stride matching her fixed foot contacts), while Orrin advances every 10 pixels (his retained 60-pixel stride). This preserves gait across adjacent cells and turns, scales to tactical/cutscene travel speeds, and stops cycling when an actor stops translating. Stopping restores the facing idle texture. Sprites previews all six poses at 80 ms each, with phase labels, pause and wraparound stepping; the Animation authoring review uses 120 ms. Attacks use 140 ms wind-up, contact through 280 ms, and recovery to 380 ms, retaining the previous battle input delay. Damage visuals, skill effects and player attack sounds begin at contact. Battle HP labels travel with their actors. Pending presentation callbacks check battle identity so forced outcomes and restarts cannot replay old hits. Retaliations are captured before round-end flag resets so counters still animate on the final turn of a round. Reduced-motion mode keeps idle artwork and shortens battle presentation.

The Vite Sprites workshop shares the same pages and frame sequence, with pause and frame stepping. Idle sprites, portraits, character rules and combat damage are unchanged. `tests/e2e/actor-motion.spec.ts` covers directions, native cropped pixels, tactical paths, both heroes' attacks and interruption. These detailed checks remain outside default CI.
