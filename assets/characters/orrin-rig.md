# Orrin's skinned walk

`src/art/orrin-actor.ts` uses the shared six-pose gait with `ORRIN_PROPORTIONS` in `src/art/rig-proportions.ts`. Two rigid 9.5-pixel leg bones, a 19.5-pixel hip baseline, 0.8-pixel rise and 28-pixel stride produce a compact stocky walk. Feet use 75% geometric size, roll around planted heel/toe contacts and retain their anatomical sides. The runtime stride in `src/content/actor-motion.ts` must match the authoring preset.

Shoulders sit 21 pixels above the hips and 11.5 pixels either side of centre. The arms have rigid 8/7-pixel bones. Orrin's right grip sits three pixels below the reference lance carry target, solved through the shared arm IK. Native directional steam-lance cutouts attach at the anatomical right grip and swing only two degrees from their source orientation. Front: viewer left. Back: viewer right. Left profile: far hand. Right profile: near hand. Neither profile is mirrored.

The four idle actors supply canonical 80 x 80 skins in `assets/characters/orrin-skin/`, retaining source RGB and thresholding alpha consistently at 128. The face, cap, goggles and beard keep their native pixels and resting registration. The static torso preserves the collar, yoke, suspenders, belt and rear tool bags; masks remove the idle near arm in each profile so it cannot create a second stationary sleeve or glove. Broad overalls fill the volume behind those moving arms. Sleeve material includes the cream fabric and exposed forearm; gloves and boots use separate native brown patches.

Orrin and Nell share the pure native skin/geometry helpers in `src/art/actor-skin.ts`. Both have continuous waist-to-thigh fabric and rounded sleeve, glove and boot shapes, rather than the reference mannequin's boxes. Orrin's wider hip spacing, fuller leg width and broad shoulders establish his own build. Changing Orrin's character model must not alter Nell's accepted walk or attacks.

Run `node scripts/export-orrin-animation.mjs` to regenerate the four 160 x 320 pages. The shared authoring pipeline writes six new walk frames and preserves Orrin's two existing attack frames byte-for-byte. No source sheets are uploaded. Repeated exports must produce identical bytes. Idle actors and portraits remain untouched. The old generated-walk importer and guides have been retired.

In Vite, open `?dev=animation` and choose **Orrin**. Review all six phases and four directions, layer hide/solo, native PNG exports, fixed-contact floor guides and pose JSON. His face/torso size and steam-lance selection stay native; other measurement controls edit his session-only configuration. **Reset proportions** restores the stocky preset. Nell, Orrin and mannequin settings remain independent and never write campaign saves. Production uses only baked pages and the authored stride; the model and source textures are dev-only.
