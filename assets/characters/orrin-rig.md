# Orrin's skinned walk

`src/art/orrin-actor.ts` uses the shared six-pose gait with `ORRIN_PROPORTIONS` in `src/art/rig-proportions.ts`. Two rigid 9.5-pixel leg bones, a 19.5-pixel hip baseline, 0.8-pixel rise and 28-pixel stride produce a compact stocky walk. Feet use 75% geometric size, roll around planted heel/toe contacts and retain their anatomical sides. The runtime stride in `src/content/actor-motion.ts` must match the authoring preset.

Shoulders sit 21 pixels above the hips and 11.5 pixels either side of centre. The arms have rigid 8/7-pixel bones. Orrin's right grip sits three pixels below the reference lance carry target, solved through the shared arm IK. Native directional steam-lance cutouts attach at the anatomical right grip and swing only two degrees from their source orientation. Front: viewer left. Back: viewer right. Left profile: far hand. Right profile: near hand. Neither profile is mirrored.

The four idle actors supply canonical 80 x 80 skins in `assets/characters/orrin-skin/`, retaining source RGB and thresholding alpha consistently at 128. The face, cap, goggles and beard keep their native pixels and resting registration. The static torso preserves the collar, yoke, suspenders, belt and rear tool bags. `ARM_SKINS` registers separate shoulder/elbow/wrist/grip landmarks and alpha spans for both arms in every direction. The native cream sleeves, rolled cuffs, bare forearms and work gloves articulate as textured cutouts, retaining their original shading and cross-section. The far profile arms use their own small source regions behind the bib, rather than a duplicate of the large near sleeve/glove. Those same spans remove every idle-arm fragment from the torso. Broad overalls fill the volume behind the moving arms. The relaxed arm binding is lowered to the resting hip baseline, so shoulders, belt and head share exactly the same body rise.

Orrin and Nell share the pure native skin/geometry helpers in `src/art/actor-skin.ts`. Both have continuous waist-to-thigh fabric and shaped boots, rather than the reference mannequin's boxes. Orrin's wider hip spacing, fuller leg width and broad shoulders establish his own build. His boots have a wider toe box and share one projected leather field across calf and shoe: brass highlights do not repeat around each mesh face. Arm thickness still adjusts the native cutouts perpendicular to each bone. The right-hand lance uses the same resting bind and rotates around its registered artwork grip; the back cutout excludes the neighbouring belt pouch. Changing Orrin's character model must not alter Nell's accepted walk or attacks.
