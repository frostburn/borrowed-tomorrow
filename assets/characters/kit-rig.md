# Kit's courier rig

`src/art/kit-actor.ts` binds the accepted four native idle actors to the layered gait. The original idle actors and portrait are unchanged. Face, hair, scarf, shirt, rolled sleeves, gloves and satchel retain native RGB pixels and native dimensions. Profiles are independent artwork; none is mirrored.

`KIT_PROPORTIONS` gives him 10-pixel leg bones, a 20.4-pixel resting hip height, 0.8-pixel rise, 3.5-pixel hip spacing, a 26-pixel stride, 70% feet, 9-pixel shoulder spacing and 8/6.5-pixel arm bones. Register the complete lower skeleton at anatomical (-0.5, -4), including every foot contact landmark. The projected fabric field follows that root through the seat and upper legs. Shaped boot shafts and shoes follow the heel/sole/toe contacts. No runtime frame fitting or mirroring is used.

Each arm has its own directional source spans and shoulder/elbow/wrist/grip registration. `actor-garment.ts` articulates those native pixels with the same helper used by Orrin. Rounded textured backing retains the sleeve/forearm/glove cross-section in foreshortened poses. Both aiming hands and the bow share the registered right-grip socket; do not apply separate idle-hand offsets to the support hand. The torso mask excludes idle arms, weapon, bag and trouser pixels; native scarf and belt remain fixed to the body. The head keeps its original expression and only follows the small hip rise.

The crossbow attaches to the anatomical right grip. Walking uses its own directional carry cutouts, lowered with a three-degree sway. The left arm counter-swings. A separately editable **Satchel** layer stays on the left hip with a small pendulum motion. Its attachment is included in pose JSON.

`kitAttackPose` braces two flat feet ten pixels apart along the facing axis. Both poses preserve those feet and rigid leg lengths. The right arm raises the trigger grip; the left reaches across to support the stock. The shared arm solver supports lateral grip offsets while preserving both bone lengths, and retains the accepted sagittal path for Nell/Orrin. Native wood samples cover the exposed crossbow stock and bow span when aiming. Wind-up/contact share the same body, limb and material rendering as the walk. Damage, bolt effects and attack sound use the shared contact timing.

Run `node scripts/export-kit-animation.mjs` with the existing Node/Vite and Python/Pillow dependencies. It prepares four 80 x 80 binary-alpha skins under `assets/characters/kit-skin/` and renders four small 160 x 320 pages under `public/art/animation/`. Frames 0–5 are the walk, 6 is wind-up and 7 is contact. It never reads an older animation page or uploads a source atlas. Stage exports and run `npm run check:art-upload` before publishing.

Animation's **Kit** skin uses the same model/renderer as the export, with layer visibility, solo, frame stepping, guides, independent measurements and PNG/JSON export. **Match Kit proportions** also fits the mannequin to his dimensions. Sprites reviews both walking and attacks. Authoring modules and skins stay dev-only; production consumes only the baked pages and the separately authored 26-pixel runtime stride.
