## Angular industrial lighting pass

`chapter_three_style.py` derives Chapter Three variants in `public/art/vector/chapter-three-kit/` from the editable originals. Roofs and window lintels meet at deliberately slanted corners. Connected factory/window bays use their world X coordinate so adjoining spans agree; broad stone and riveted steel courses use unequal straight runs. The cavern has a fractured rear face instead of a rectangular wall. Stairs, rail mechanics, vessel facets, gauge centers, source dimensions, numeric tile IDs and scene anchors remain exact. Northglass's review-layer names now describe the factory and pressure dome. The later royal marble sources are untouched.

`src/content/chapter-three/lighting.json` registers cool green ambient/bounce, warm outdoor lamps and window apertures, uncovered indoor opal work lights, pale instrument faces and the restrained crystal. The compiler paints floor-clipped spill from the same sources; the game and Maps apply native actor highlight planes and up to three overlapping silhouette shadows. All eight compiled layouts have profiles. Indoor working areas use high ambient illumination, restrained diffuse local light, low-contrast floor planes and a 0.035 total cast-shadow opacity cap with equally faint contact shadows. Indoor window light has no spotlight cones. Exterior light and shadows remain unchanged. The actual upward-facing regulator chamber also has uncovered opal work lights, a brighter steel deck and a matching native floor shadow mask; the accepted boss body and mechanisms are unchanged. Its campaign and workshop share the same lighting. The Chapter Seven collector reprise keeps its separate art.

The conduit Feed light layer is marked `live`: it stays independently addressable by the Emperor's shutdown effect. `drawTileMap` caches consecutive static ground groups on either side, preserving the live layer's exact draw order. Flags and restores rebuild those caches. This retains the earlier performance improvement without flattening animated scenery. No extra idle repaint loop, raster asset, production dependency or save-format change is introduced.
