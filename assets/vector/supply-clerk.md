# Supply clerk

`public/art/vector/supply-clerk.svg` is an editable native 80 × 80 SVG, derived from the existing blue traveler's vector construction with a green work coat, linen apron, cap and stock ledger. Keep its (40,68) floor anchor and civilian rendering style. It is registered as an unnamed civilian, independent of Iona's `clerk` identity. Tiled rest interactions own the clerk assignments; `map_metadata.py` preserves them during scenery exports. Existing named attendants remain unchanged.
