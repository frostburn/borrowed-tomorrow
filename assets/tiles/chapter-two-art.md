# Harbor and railway ward props

Built-in Image Gen produced six isolated transparent props using the accepted Chapter One modules as a visual reference. The large authoring sheet stays outside the repository. Only real native 24px tile pieces are committed; no source-sheet fragments are uploaded.

The shared catalog appends four lossless WebP pages, `public/art/chapter-one/tiles-060.webp` through `tiles-063.webp`. Every page is at most 192 × 192 pixels and 128 KB. The exporter verifies identical decoded RGBA pixels before writing. Existing page names, ranges and bytes are preserved.

Run `python3 assets/tiles/import-chapter-two.py /path/to/scenery.png` with Pillow for authoring. The crop rectangles, native budgets and physical foot rows are in `chapter-two-scenery.json`; registered anchors are in `modules.json`. The importer removes detached alpha fragments, retains the source colors, samples once with nearest-neighbor at the native budget and pads to 24px. Normal builds use the checked-in pages directly.

The crane, coal shed, iron bed, medicine desk, windowed plaster/dado wall and stove match the existing slate roofs, warm brass and outlined pixel clusters. Physical foot tiles block movement; upper pieces share a ground depth so actors sort around the whole prop.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE modular scenery texture sheet for the JRPG Borrowed Tomorrow, not a map. Input image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its full-color illustrated pixel scenery, crisp tiny detail, slightly elevated straight-on orthographic camera, dark outlines, slate shadows, weathered warm wood, brass, softly illuminated edges and believable rounded three-dimensional volume. Generate a transparent 1536 by 1024 sheet, six isolated props in exactly THREE COLUMNS and TWO ROWS, each confined to its own 512 by 512 cell with generous transparent gutters. Row 1 left: a compact rusty railway harbor loading crane with stone base, iron lattice upright and horizontal jib/chain, fully visible. Row 1 middle: a small slate-roofed coal shed, dark open coal bay, timber and corrugated iron walls, warm window, facing the camera like the reference house. Row 1 right: one empty railway hospital bed, iron frame, cream pillow, white bedding and folded muted ochre blanket, head away from the camera, long axis vertical. Row 2 left: wooden medicine desk with ledger, small glass bottles, bandages, front visible, no writing. Row 2 middle: ONE straight repeatable hospital back-wall segment, eight native tiles wide and four high in proportion, aged warm plaster/stone, two tall dark mullioned windows and a blue lower dado, exposed wall top, no roof, straight flat horizontal ends so copies join. Row 2 right: a small cast-iron ward stove with short flue and warm fire through its door. Consistent soft light from upper left. Each prop centered toward its cell bottom, no shared floor, no floating detached fragments, no people, no labels or symbols, no text, no checkerboard painted into the transparency. This is charming substantial railway fantasy scenery at the reference's detail level, not flat 8-bit symbols or photographic rendering. The full sheet remains an authoring source; it will be exported into small native tile pieces.
